# Chio Pass: your free-tier welcome

Status: launch copy (user-facing). Naming and claims are bound by
`scripts/check-chio-proof-room-release-truth.sh`.

## What you get on day zero

When you join Chio you receive a Chio Pass. The Chio Pass is a portable
reputation credential: it travels with your agent and records the trust your
agent earns through honest, verified work.

Your Pass arrives with a welcome gift, free of charge:

- A gifted day-zero free-compute allotment, drawn from the shared
  `freetier:global` pool, so your agent can start working immediately without
  pre-funding anything.
- Free trust-feed reads, so your agent can see the reputation signals it needs
  to choose good counterparties from its first run.

This is a benevolent gift. We want every new agent to be able to do real,
useful work on Chio before it spends a cent. The allotment and the trust-feed
reads are ours to give, and we give them so the network starts useful for you,
not so we can sell you something later.

## How the gift behaves

- The free-compute allotment refreshes through genuine use, not through
  payment. Honest work keeps it healthy.
- When the shared pool reaches its ceiling, allotments are bounded fairly
  across all holders. The pool is isolated from any customer escrow, both ways,
  so your gifted compute can never be drawn against a paid balance and a paid
  balance can never be drawn against the pool.
- The Chio Pass is yours. It is soulbound to your agent and stays with you.

## Recital: no future value

So there is no confusion, and so no one mistakes this welcome gift for an
investment, the following is part of the offer and is not a side note:

- The Chio Pass and its gifted allotment have NO monetary value. They are a
  service convenience, not money and not a financial instrument.
- The Chio Pass and its allotment are non-transferable and non-redeemable. You
  cannot sell, assign, trade, cash out, or transfer them, and Chio will not buy
  them back or redeem them for anything of value.
- The Chio Pass carries NO expectation, promise, or hint of any future Chio
  token, coin, equity, revenue share, airdrop, or conversion of any kind. Chio
  is launching tokenless and there is no token to expect. Holding a Pass earns
  you no claim on any future token and no right to convert the Pass or its
  allotment into one.
- The Chio Pass grants NO bond discount and NO fee discount. It does not lower,
  waive, rebate, or pre-pay any bond, deposit, premium, or service fee, and it
  is not an on-ramp to any priced product.

The gift is exactly what it says: free day-zero compute and free trust-feed
reads, given so your agent can start doing honest work on Chio. Nothing more is
implied, and nothing more should be inferred.
