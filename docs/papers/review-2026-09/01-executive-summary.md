# Executive Summary

Review of the two Chio whitepapers at `main` `fe5657020c`, 2026-09-10 to 2026-09-11. Method, waves, and pinned facts are in `00-method-and-scope.md`. Everything below cites a finding id (`findings.json`: 172 findings, 121 confirmed and 44 adjusted by independent verifiers, 7 added by the completeness critic, none refuted; 19 blockers, 67 major, 57 minor, 29 notes) or a command the reader can rerun.

## The answer

Neither paper is close to a foundational document, and they fail in opposite ways. P1 (Receiver-Owned Bilateral Admission) is true of the code and has no idea in it that a reader can hold; it is organized around evidence surfaces rather than around a mechanism, contains no worked example and no wire format, hedges its two disclaimers nine and four times, and its measurement record is two runs spliced together. P2 (Bilateral Receipt Admission) has the shape of a foundational document, one named primitive with a worked envelope and an attack-by-construction section, and it is false: its gate order, rejection-code taxonomy, wire format, subject-digest definition, trust-store model, and two of its defended attacks do not exist at HEAD, and its Lean and benchmark claims are wrong. On the ten-criterion foundational-paper rubric (`06-foundational-paper-benchmark.md`) P1 scores 20 of 50 and P2 21 of 50. Six simulated program-committee reviewers, reading the papers as submitted without repository access, gave every review a 2 of 5; five would accept at a workshop, none at a top-tier venue (`07-simulated-pc-reviews.md`).

The good news is that the gap is not in the code. The mechanism P1 describes is real, hash-stable, and passes every declared gate at HEAD (`02-p1-claim-audit.md`), the formal estate around it is larger than either paper uses (`04-formal-evidence-audit.md`), and the territory Chio occupies is genuinely unclaimed by MCP, A2A, the payment protocols, the policy engines, or the transparency-log standards (`09-claim-surface-and-landscape.md`). What is missing is one paper with one idea, stated plainly, delivered exactly, and measured once on one machine.

## The ten most consequential findings

1. **P1's numbers come from two machines and the paper describes a third state.** Committed PDFs and claim ledger print the 26 July Linux run (11.1 ms p50 denial); the retained result files and the TeX macros print a later MacBook Pro run (4.1 ms); the prose describes the Linux server. One commit, `0c01448309` on 2026-08-01, swapped the runs and truncated the environment record; the prose, ledger, and abstract were never updated. The artifact gate never compares prose to results and is red at HEAD. The lead reviewer's rerun on the Linux host gave 10.4 ms, confirming the Linux run and not the files the paper compiles from. (BENCH1-01, BENCH1-02, BENCH1-03, IMPL1-05, IMPL1-06; reproduction table in `02-p1-claim-audit.md`)
2. **P1's headline "full pre-dispatch denial path" never verifies a signature.** The timed fixture's unanimous-deny policy summary makes the hook return `chio_treaty_policy_denied` before the treaty-binding checks and before the Ed25519 verification call in `admission_hook/dsse.rs`. The number is real but it is an early exit dominated by the SQLite receipt append, and the admitted path through the hook is not measured anywhere. (BENCH1-09, raised to blocker by the verifier; BENCH1-10; confirmed by the lead reviewer)
3. **P2 describes a verifier that does not exist.** Signatures are verified last, not first; there are 16 rejection codes, not five, and none has the paper's names; the worked envelope would fail decoding at the first gate; the subject digest is the receipt body, not a hashed tuple; the trust store is symmetric, not issuer-versus-kernel. (IMPL2-01, IMPL2-05, IMPL2-08, IMPL2-09, IMPL2-12; independently XREF-02, XREF-03, PROSE-01 to PROSE-04)
4. **Two of P2's six "attacks defeated by construction" have no mechanism.** No treaty carries a multi-lane witness quorum and the verifier evaluates no anchors; no operator-attribution metadata exists, so one operator with two keys passes, exactly as P1's PS-A-01 says. (IMPL2-13, IMPL2-16, FORM-15, HARN-01, HARN-02, XREF-05, XREF-06)
5. **P2's formal claims are false and P1's are shallow.** P2 over-reports its axioms (the four theorems depend on `propext` alone), describes a version of the Lean file replaced on 2026-07-12, and calls a two-line definitional bi-conditional load-bearing. P1's two theorems are accurate and honest, but they are `List.all` characterizations of a checker that is not on the authorization path, and the module is registered in none of the repository's proof registries. (FORM-01 to FORM-06, FORM-10, HARN-04, HARN-05)
6. **P1 misattributes its own verifier.** Section 5 presents `verify_chio_bilateral_invocation` (peer pins, live manifests, revocation epoch) as the pre-dispatch verifier; its only production caller is the offline buyer verifier. The hook uses the envelope verifier plus its own binding checks. Six of the twenty matrix cases do not call the surfaces the paper says they call. (IMPL1-02, IMPL1-03)
7. **The repository names the fifth ladder mode four different ways.** Spec and federation crate: `maintenance`; runtime-core: `maintenance` or the alias `quorum_required`; Lean: `quorumRequired`; P1: "quorum-required", which in the spec is a consistency model. A manifest accepted by runtime-core can be rejected by the federation crate for both mode and co-sign vocabulary. (IMPL1-01, FORM-11, XREF-07; IMPL1 verifier note 1)
8. **The treaty path has no Kani, Creusot, fuzz, or mutation coverage, and its strongest existing evidence is uncited.** The 21-test byte-mutation conformance suite over the real rejection codes, the kernel tests showing forged treaty metadata cannot reach dispatch or the cosigner, and the Apalache release-or-retain models are all in-tree, run on every PR, and appear in neither paper. (HARN-12 to HARN-17)
9. **The normative spec is stale against the code in four places** (two predicate URIs versus one; n-of-m signature count; five code-name disagreements; step order), and the ladder spec describes an unmapped-class fallback the runtime does not implement. Any rewrite must pick the code and say so. (XREF-11, XREF-12)
10. **The six-paper family is internally inconsistent.** P2 and the four siblings lean on a "polity-layer construction" that no longer exists as a paper; the reversible-action Lean draft does not compile and its headline theorem is `sorry`; the family cites the parent under three titles; P2 defers to an anonymized companion fourteen times while naming the project in its affiliation. (XREF-09, XREF-10, XREF-17, XREF-19, FORM-14)

Two further items from the completeness critic: P2's claim that every denial appears as a signed canonical receipt is an overclaim, since envelope-level and validator denials, including all 20 matrix cases, return typed errors with no receipt, and only the kernel dispatch path signs a denial (CRIT-01); and neither paper cites the repository's assumption registry ids, so a reader cannot tell that the papers' trust boundary is the audited one (CRIT-05).

A statistical note that a reviewer would raise before any of these: with 20 and 30 samples every reported p99 is the largest sample, component samples are batch means, and no confidence interval is reported although the retained July log shows a 46 percent run-to-run swing. (BENCH1-07, BENCH1-08)

## What is strong and can be built on

- A pre-dispatch hook that resolves every artifact from receiver-owned stores, rejects request-borne trust material, consumes a continuation once through a primary-key insert, and hands accepted treaty material to receipt construction as a validated type that is rechecked. All of this is tested, and forged runtime treaty metadata cannot reach dispatch or the cosigner. (`02-p1-claim-audit.md`, "What is true and strong")
- A claim discipline (claim ledger, assumptions registry, disallowed-wording registry, symbol-level artifact manifest) that is better than the field's norm and should be kept, but moved into one assumptions paragraph so the paper can speak plainly everywhere else.
- A formal estate of 165 theorems, Aeneas extraction of 20 kernel-core functions, 41 public Kani harnesses, Apalache models, and mutation campaigns, which the surviving paper should describe once, as layered tripwires with named survivors, never as a verified kernel.
- Unclaimed territory: no competitor defines the receipt that is simultaneously the receiver-verified admission evidence, the unit of account, and the object whose attenuation, revocation, and inclusion semantics have machine-checked evidence. (`09-claim-surface-and-landscape.md`)

## The recommended direction

Write one paper, not two, and not a polish of either. Its spine is the rule the thesis panel converged on from five independent angles (`08-the-thesis.md`): authority is minted and verified only inside the kernel that dispatches, against roots that kernel activated itself, and nothing carried in a request can add a root; only signed, content-addressed evidence crosses an organization boundary. Its named primitive is the receipt in two forms: the kernel-signed, content-addressed record of every admission decision, written durably before the caller sees the result and carrying what the call cost and who paid; and the co-signed receipt, a statement whose single subject is the digest of a receipt the receiver already holds, signed by exactly two keys the receiver pinned before the request arrived, binding the treaty, the request hash, and a single-use continuation. A cross-organization call is the same admission with the peer kernel's receipt and co-signature supplied as evidence, never as authority. The security argument is four definitions (admission binding, receiver locality, single use, audience binding) with reductions to Ed25519 unforgeability, SHA-256 collision resistance, JCS injectivity, and single-row store atomicity, each tied to a registered assumption id, and a substitution table over the real binding fields naming the code that fires. Length eight to ten pages, one real envelope and one real denial receipt, one measured number with its environment file, the assumptions table once, no research questions, no contribution bullets, no crate names, no sibling-paper deferrals, no polity or sovereignty vocabulary.

The preconditions, in order, are in `10-roadmap.md`. The two that cannot be skipped: re-pin the artifact on one machine with results, prose, ledger, and PDFs regenerated from one run; and run the one experiment every candidate thesis needs and none has, two separately administered kernels on two hosts with disjoint stores and keys, over the existing iroh lanes, driving the 20-case negative corpus over the wire with the receiver's dispatch counter asserted zero and the admitted path measured. If that experiment cannot be run, the paper must say cross-organization admission has been exercised only in-process, and the claim retreats from "no shared kernel" to "receiver-owned admission". The rule survives either way; the demonstration does not.
