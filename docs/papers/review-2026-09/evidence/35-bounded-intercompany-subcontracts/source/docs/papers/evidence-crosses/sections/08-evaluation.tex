\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

Local measurements ran on one machine: \PSHostCores{} \PSHostCpuModel{} cores,
\PSHostMemoryGiB{} GiB, \PSHostOs{}, compiler \PSRustcVersion{} at the pinned
toolchain. Percentiles interpolate between order statistics; each interval
below is a percentile bootstrap interval for its median.
These timings come from retained runs predating the new lease and governance
activation checks; they are not latency measurements of the revised hook.

\paragraph{Two kernels in separate processes.}
Two kernels ran as separate operating-system processes, each with its own keys,
stores, agreements, and receipt log, a third process driving calls over
\PSFedTransport{}; the topology record reports \PSFedHosts{} hosts.
The co-signing and revocation hops are the shipped lanes unchanged; the request
hop uses a transport defined for this experiment, since no shipped lane carries
a tool call. Admitted calls: \PSFedAllowSampleCount{}
calls, p50 \PSFedAllowPFiftyMs{} ms, interval \PSFedAllowCiLowMs{} to
\PSFedAllowCiHighMs{} ms, p99 \PSFedAllowPNinetyNineMs{} ms at the driver, each
costing \PSFedCosignConnections{} co-signing connections, counted by the
receiver. The round trip is the receiver's decision plus the co-signing hops it
makes back to the origin after dispatching, inside its
\PSFedReceiverEvalPFiftyMs{} ms median evaluation window; minting the per-call
evidence costs \PSFedEvidencePrepPFiftyMs{} ms more, outside it.

Denied calls: \PSFedDenyCases{} scenarios over the wire,
\PSFedDenySampleCount{} calls, p50 \PSFedDenyPFiftyMs{} ms, p99
\PSFedDenyPNinetyNineMs{} ms. They return \code{chio_treaty_missing_scope},
\code{chio_treaty_scope_hash_mismatch},
\code{chio_treaty_missing_intersection},
\code{chio_treaty_intersection_mismatch},
\code{chio_treaty_missing_required_evidence},
\code{request_smuggled_trust_root}, and
\code{request_smuggled_dynamic_trust}. In every one the receiver's dispatch
counter stayed zero. \PSFedAbsorbedCount{} calls were retried and excluded, denied on a snapshot aged
past the \PSFreshnessMs{} ms freshness window against an origin publishing
every \PSEpochTickMs{} ms.

Revoking at the origin and measuring to the receiver's first denial took
\PSFedRevokeToDenyMs{} ms at the median over \PSFedRevokeRepeats{} repeats,
interval \PSFedRevokeCiLowMs{} to \PSFedRevokeCiHighMs{} ms, a median of
\PSFedRevokeCallsMedian{} calls. With the link cut the receiver serves until its
snapshot leaves the freshness window and then denies, \PSFedCutToDenyMs{} ms at
the median over \PSFedCutRepeats{} repeats, interval \PSFedCutCiLowMs{} to
\PSFedCutCiHighMs{} ms, a median of \PSFedCutCallsMedian{} calls, counting a
denial only once it persists for three further calls. On
\PSFedRevokeAmbiguousReleases{} of \PSFedRevokeDenialRecords{} revoke records
and \PSFedCutAmbiguousReleases{} of \PSFedCutDenialRecords{} cut records the
denial also reports an unconfirmed pre-dispatch cleanup: the budget unwind
rather than the continuation release Section~\ref{sec:admission} orders,
reporting a budget hold it cannot find, an invariant violation we do not
explain. No call dispatched.

Table~\ref{tab:baseline} prices what an operator adds;
Table~\ref{tab:carriers} says the explicit fields this profile omits. Hardening closes
\PSBaseClosedByHardening{} attacks and carries \PSBasePropsHoldHardened{} of the
four properties, and \PSBaseHardenedAdmits{} attacks survive it. The field ledger describes this profile, whose authorization server is
controlled by the caller. It does not bound OAuth, JWT, or A2A: OAuth permits a
receiver-owned issuer and opaque token lookup, and JWT permits application
claims. A regression using the unchanged receiver and token types installs a
separate receiver-owned issuer. Receiver issuance admits once; replay under a
new message identifier and a caller-signed replacement both deny. This
counterexample establishes issuer-placement flexibility, not complete binding
or crash-recovery parity for the treaty protocol.

An independent opaque-handle ledger and Chio's experimental effect slots
receive identical evidence, owner rules, verifiers, SQLite durability and
effect sinks. They match in sixteen cases, six state transitions, eight-process
races and four kill boundaries per gate. Both publish once and reject
replacement agents; uncertain post-claim effects remain unknown.
Both also publish a checked Git-hook repair. An offline auditor rejects signed
buggy source and accepts unsigned repairs without proving publication history.
A three-receiver archival graph matches in eleven scenarios and six attacks,
with twenty process kills. Namespaces isolate roles; the host and operator
remain trusted. No integration-cost advantage is established.

A separate work profile connects a Python buyer, Rust provider and isolated
Python procurement worker to a Rust specialist over HTTPS. Four distinct keys
separate agents from kernel issuers. Buyer-approved authentication declarations
alone cross to the specialist. Its kernel requires a provider-signed permit
for the exact child job; a delegated key alone cannot buy another job.
Both verifiers check nested delivery evidence. After a parent crash, agreed
release can restore the buyer's credit while the specialist remains paid.
These local-credit observations establish neither atomic settlement across
companies nor independently operated deployment.

\paragraph{Verification by someone who was not there.}
The \PSProofPackageKiB{} KiB evidence package for the refund workflow verifies
every receipt, statement, lease, and governance record in it with no kernel
access, in \fpeval{round(\PSBuyerVerifyPFiftyUs/1000,1)}\,ms at the median over
\PSBuyerVerifySampleCount{} runs. A frozen corpus of
\PSVectorCaseCount{} receipt cases holds \PSVectorLanguages{} independent
implementations to the same canonical body bytes and verdict.

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
The retained benchmark runs include per-host commit and environment records,
raw samples and generated numeric macros checked against those artifacts.
The historical composition benchmark records timings only with a clean input
tree and an unloaded host. The newer outcome-gate experiment retains source
hashes, input artifacts, receiver snapshots and test logs from an uncommitted
local tree; it makes no timing claim.
