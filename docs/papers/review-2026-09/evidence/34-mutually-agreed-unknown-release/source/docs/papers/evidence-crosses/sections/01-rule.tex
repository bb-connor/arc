\section{The rule}

A buyer agent at one company asks a vendor at a second to stage a refund.
The vendor must decide before touching its ledger whether these arguments,
under this agreement, remain authorized and whether the permitted effect was
already attempted. Caller identity alone does not answer those questions.
Retries and replacement agents must not manufacture another authorization.

\dfn{Effective authority is decided inside the kernel that dispatches, against
roots the receiver activated itself; nothing carried in a request can add a
root. Signed, content-addressed evidence crosses the organization boundary.}
The kernel may trust configured capability issuers, but a credential has effect
only through this local decision. The receiver owns its active agreements,
pinned keys, validity windows, budgets, and continuation-consumption state.
This applies established end-to-end and trust-management principles
\cite{saltzer1984,keynote,howell2000} to the dispatch boundary.

A kernel-signed receipt records the decision. An inbound co-signed statement
names receiver-held evidence and gates admission. An outbound statement binds
the newly durable receipt and gates the response; its countersigner reconstructs
the typed preimage and checks participant identities and key fingerprints.
That check does not independently reproduce the receiver's policy evaluation.
Authorization, outcome, and accounting records share cryptographic bindings;
the property does not require them to be one physical object.

The interface specifies which evidence is authenticated, which local state it
resolves, and when that state is consumed. Some facts travel as explicit
references; others can be recovered through a receiver-issued opaque handle.
OAuth permits that design \cite{rfc6749,rfc8693}. Our comparison measures one
selected profile and its omitted bindings, not the expressive limits of those
formats. The research question is whether independent organizations can
procure, delegate, verify and settle agent work through a common contract while
retaining local authority. Discovery, messaging and market evidence must bind
to that contract. Our experiments establish local implementation properties;
independent deployment and reduced application-specific recovery remain open.
Python--Rust work, incidents and agreed releases establish neither
independent operation nor novelty.
