\section{Programmable sovereignty}
\label{sec:sovereignty}
A kernel for agentic operating systems must give an untrusted planner useful
freedom while preserving the owner's control over effects. In Chio, the kernel
is the trusted mediator for tool access and its durable records. Host isolation
and tool confinement supply the surrounding enforcement boundary.

An \emph{owner} creates a bounded work allocation and qualifies the principals
that may use it. A \emph{holder} can subdivide its allocation and select a
provider. A \emph{receiver} issues the capability for its tool and decides
whether to admit the invocation. A \emph{payer} backs a payment obligation in a
chosen settlement domain, and a \emph{verifier} applies the agreed acceptance
procedure. These are roles, not a requirement for six organizations. Couriers
carry signed evidence without acquiring the authority of its signers.

\subsection{Authority stays with the resource owner}
A work agreement expresses what parties intend to exchange. Admission decides
whether a particular invocation may use a particular receiver's resources.
The receiver loads trusted issuers, policy, treaty records and replay state from
its own protected stores. Request metadata cannot install those authorities.

A bilateral treaty supplies the conditions under which the parties cooperate.
The receiver checks the configured participants and their signatures over the
same canonical statement, its permitted action, validity and continuation, and
the references binding it to this invocation. These checks join the receiver's
ordinary capability, revocation and guard checks. A valid work permit, treaty
or funded allocation satisfies its own part of admission; none substitutes
for the other required decisions.

The distinction also governs change. An owner can refuse a fresh invocation
under its current policy. Replaying an admitted operation resolves its existing
history. A claim already earned in escrow follows the immutable terms under
which the payer funded it. Sovereignty determines which commitments an owner
enters; the commitment's lifecycle determines how it is discharged.

A sealed allowance is not a deposit. An authenticated execution statement is
not a payment decision. A financial refund is not proof that a tool had no
effect. Keeping these facts distinct is what allows the domains to compose:
each contributes a decision it is actually authorized to make.

\subsection{Trust and failure model}
Agents and couriers may be malicious. Messages may be duplicated, reordered,
delayed or lost, and processes may crash between durable steps. Safety assumes
unforgeable signatures, collision-resistant canonical digests, valid clock
checks, and honest enforcement by the selected resource custodians. Each
delegation root has one protected allocator; each declared swarm pool has one
protected graph lineage. Their custodians serialize allocation and head
changes. Each receiving key has one protected execution and budget domain.
The rail serializes financial transitions and couples them to actual asset
transfers.

These are local trust requirements. Cloning a signing key and its database into
independently spendable forks, or provisioning two graph lineages against the
same declared pool, violates them. Tools must be confined well enough
for their declared effects and readers to describe the protected operation.
The acceptance authority and settlement observation must also be trustworthy.

The implemented discovery step selects among previously enrolled principals.
A new result can create work and select its receiver; it cannot authorize a new
reader or install a new trust root. This paper's Rust profiles instantiate
delegation, additive graph evolution and funded claims, identified in the
artifact as D1, S1 and F1. Appendix~\ref{app:profiles} fixes their concrete
encodings and limits.

\emph{Verifiable work} means work bound to an agreed check and authenticated
evidence. The kernel's receipt attests its own mediated execution; the selected
verifier decides the agreed acceptance predicate. Their signatures make those
statements attributable and checkable. The value of the work depends on what
the predicate measures.
