\section{Relation to existing mechanisms}
Contract Net already organizes distributed work through announcements, bids
and awards~\cite{contractnet}. Agent Contracts unifies task specifications,
resource constraints and success conditions, including delegated budget
conservation~\cite{agentcontracts}. Chio does not introduce contracting or
hierarchical budget discipline. Our focus is the boundary between a remote
operator's local budget and an obligation backed by a settlement authority the
operator cannot rewrite.

AgentBound places deterministic governance before execution and binds decisions
to verifiable receipts~\cite{agentbound}. That overlaps substantially with local
authorization and audit. A receipt about permitted behavior does not by itself
reserve funds at a separate authority or decide application-specific output
acceptance. Chio's work contract explicitly joins those duties, while retaining
their distinct assumptions.

A2A supplies tasks, messages, artifacts and discovery~\cite{a2a}; AP2 specifies
signed commercial and payment mandates and their verification~\cite{ap2}.
AP2 v0.2 leaves shopping-agent mandate delegation outside its scope and permits
extensible authorization models. That is a scope choice, not evidence that its
carriers cannot express this work contract. Likewise, OAuth token exchange
leaves significant trust and token semantics to deployment profiles~\cite{oauth}.
A receiver-issued credential and ordinary signed application fields can enforce
receiver-owned authorization. Interoperability requires a defined profile and
conformance tests, not a claim that other formats lack expressive power.

ERC-8183 is a particularly close construction: it specifies escrowed agent jobs,
providers, evaluator-approved completion and expiry refunds~\cite{erc8183}.
The economic pattern is already present. The pinned upstream implementation
additionally supports partial claims and relayable EIP-712 authorization; the
specification and implementation differ in details. Section 6 tests their
overlap with Chio directly. No new economic primitive follows from separating
acceptance and withdrawal or from independently funding child jobs.
ERC-8004 provides discovery, reputation and validation interfaces while leaving
payments to other protocols~\cite{erc8004}. A registry entry is evidence a
receiver may consider, not a capability to use its resources. Both standards
are drafts at the pinned snapshots.

Sagas explain how sequences of transactions use application-defined
compensation~\cite{sagas}. A refund is such a financial action; it need not erase
an already delivered effect. Our explicit unknown state prevents recovery from
inventing a successful execution or a no-effect history. Durable workflows and
transactional outboxes remain appropriate competing implementation techniques.

Contingent service payments and fair exchange have established constructions
for linking payment to verification~\cite{zkcsp,fairswap}. F1 is weaker in an
important respect: it relies on a selected verifier, rather than supplying a
cryptographic proof of arbitrary useful work or complete fair exchange.
Replacing that verifier with a proof system would require a restricted task
language, cost measurements and a new analysis of availability and disclosure.
The current implementation does not establish that extension.

The relevant comparison is thus a composition of competent implementations of
these mechanisms. A missing field in a base specification is not an obstacle
to that composition. Chio offers a particular contract and enforcement path;
whether that common contract materially lowers the cost of changing partners
remains the experiment that would distinguish protocol value from a large
bespoke integration.
