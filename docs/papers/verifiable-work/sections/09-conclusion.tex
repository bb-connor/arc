\section{Conclusion}
Chio composes work by fixing the selected call, preserving its execution right
as the plan grows, and paying accepted work from its own reserved funds. Each
step is checked and recorded by the authority that controls the affected
resource. Recovery follows those records, so a lost response cannot create a
fresh allowance, renew an invocation or cancel an earned payment.

These operations form a kernel interface for the agentic web. An agent can
discover tasks and recruit collaborators during execution, while each receiving
owner determines what may enter its domain. The program's control flow may be
distributed and discovered at runtime; enforcement remains with the owners of
the tools and funds it uses.
