\section{Conclusion}
A work relationship should bind permission to act, a rule for accepting the
result and a funded obligation, while leaving each receiver in control of its
resources. Chio makes those bindings explicit and preserves them through local
execution, evidence exchange and financial recovery. Separately funded,
recorded earned claims survive a parent's failure under the stated settlement
and availability assumptions.

The mechanism has a concrete implementation and falsifiable boundaries. Its
monetary rule is reproducible with ordinary escrow, and its strongest proposed
benefit is still to be measured: making independently operated work
relationships portable at lower integration cost. The protocol supplies a
basis for that experiment and a precise account of what the experiment must
preserve.
