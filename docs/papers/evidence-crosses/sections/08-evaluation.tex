\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

Local measurements ran on one machine: \PSHostCores{} \PSHostCpuModel{} cores,
\PSHostMemoryGiB{} GiB, \PSHostOs{}, compiler \PSRustcVersion{} at the pinned
toolchain. Percentiles interpolate between order statistics; each interval
below is a percentile bootstrap interval for its median.

\paragraph{Two kernels in separate processes.}
Two kernels ran as separate operating-system processes, each with its own keys,
stores, agreements, and receipt log, with a third process driving calls over
\PSFedTransport{}; the topology record reports a host count of \PSFedHosts{}.
The co-signing and revocation hops are the shipped lanes unchanged; the request
hop uses a transport defined for this experiment, since no shipped lane carries
a tool call. Admitted calls: \PSFedAllowSampleCount{}
calls, p50 \PSFedAllowPFiftyMs{} ms, interval \PSFedAllowCiLowMs{} to
\PSFedAllowCiHighMs{} ms, p99 \PSFedAllowPNinetyNineMs{} ms at the driver, each
costing \PSFedCosignConnections{} co-signing connections, counted by the
receiver. The round trip is the receiver's decision plus the co-signing hops it
makes back to the origin after dispatching; its evaluation window,
\PSFedReceiverEvalPFiftyMs{} ms at the median, contains those hops. Minting the
per-call evidence costs the receiver \PSFedEvidencePrepPFiftyMs{} ms more,
outside it.

Denied calls: \PSFedDenyCases{} scenarios over the wire,
\PSFedDenySampleCount{} calls, p50 \PSFedDenyPFiftyMs{} ms, p99
\PSFedDenyPNinetyNineMs{} ms. They return \code{chio_treaty_missing_scope},
\code{chio_treaty_scope_hash_mismatch},
\code{chio_treaty_missing_intersection},
\code{chio_treaty_intersection_mismatch},
\code{chio_treaty_missing_required_evidence},
\code{request_smuggled_trust_root}, and
\code{request_smuggled_dynamic_trust}. In every one the receiver's dispatch
counter stayed zero. \PSFedAbsorbedCount{} calls were retried and excluded, denied because the receiver's snapshot had aged past its
\PSFreshnessMs{} ms freshness window against an origin publishing every
\PSEpochTickMs{} ms.

Revoking at the origin and measuring to the receiver's first denial took
\PSFedRevokeToDenyMs{} ms at the median over \PSFedRevokeRepeats{} repeats,
interval \PSFedRevokeCiLowMs{} to \PSFedRevokeCiHighMs{} ms, a median of
\PSFedRevokeCallsMedian{} calls. With the link cut the receiver serves until its
snapshot leaves the freshness window and then denies, \PSFedCutToDenyMs{} ms at
the median over \PSFedCutRepeats{} repeats, interval \PSFedCutCiLowMs{} to
\PSFedCutCiHighMs{} ms, a median of \PSFedCutCallsMedian{} calls, counting a
denial only once it persists for three further calls. On
\PSFedRevokeAmbiguousReleases{} of \PSFedRevokeDenialRecords{} revoke records
and \PSFedCutAmbiguousReleases{} of \PSFedCutDenialRecords{} cut records the
denial also reports an unconfirmed pre-dispatch cleanup: the budget unwind
rather than the continuation release Section~\ref{sec:admission} orders,
reporting a budget hold it cannot find, an invariant violation we do not
explain. No call dispatched.

The shape of Table~\ref{tab:local} is the result: the cryptography is not the
expense. Appending the receipt durably costs
\fpeval{round(\PSReceiptAppendPFiftyUs/\PSBilateralVerifyPFiftyUs,1)} times what
verifying the inbound statement costs, and
\fpeval{round(\PSReceiptAppendPFiftyUs/\PSCrossBoundaryPFiftyUs,0)} times the
receiver's decision over already resolved evidence, where the comparisons of
Table~\ref{tab:subst} run. Durability dominates, which is the price of
Section~\ref{sec:admission}'s ordering. The refusal row is a lower bound: its
fixture refuses on a unanimous deny verdict, an early exit reaching neither
those comparisons nor the signature checks. Driven back to back for
\PSSustainedSeconds{} s with one call outstanding, the same path admitted
\PSSustainedCalls{} calls with \PSSustainedDenials{} denials and
\fpeval{round(\PSSustainedReceiptStoreGrowthKiB/1024,1)}\,MiB of receipt-store
growth: one call's cost repeated, not a capacity.

The negative corpus is \PSThreatCaseCount{} in-process cases, each asserting a
refused decision and its expected code: \PSThreatDispatchCaseCount{} through the
dispatch-capable hook, \PSThreatVerifierCaseCount{} against the verifier
directly, alongside \PSBilateralReplayCases{} bilateral and
\PSGenericReplayCases{} generic replay cases.

Table~\ref{tab:baseline} prices what an operator adds. Hardening closes
\PSBaseClosedByHardening{} attacks and carries \PSBasePropsHoldHardened{} of the
four properties. Attacks divide by what closes them: a check over fields the
request carries, or a field it does not have. Only the second is about the
format: \PSBaseSubstNotCarriedRows{} of the \PSBaseSubstRows{} substitution rows
have no carrier at all, one of them the report digest Chio never compares
either. The residual witness, an unmodified admissible call against a receiver
whose record for that agreement has advanced a version, is closed by a version
field; the leaf enumeration of Section~\ref{sec:formal} counts the rest, so the
carrier is settled by the decision, not added to it. Hardening is not free:
the last row prices the second durable write it makes.

\paragraph{Verification by someone who was not there.}
The \PSProofPackageKiB{} KiB evidence package for the refund workflow verifies
every receipt, statement, lease, and governance record in it with no kernel
access, in \fpeval{round(\PSBuyerVerifyPFiftyUs/1000,1)}\,ms at the median over
\PSBuyerVerifySampleCount{} runs. A frozen corpus of
\PSVectorCaseCount{} receipt cases holds \PSVectorLanguages{} independent
implementations to the same canonical body bytes and verdict.

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
Each run writes per-host commit and environment records, raw samples, and the
macro file this paper compiles from, so a printed number and a retained one
cannot drift. The comparison writes a duration only from a run on a clean input
tree and an unloaded host, and otherwise records that it did not: its corpora
and verdicts are above, its timings are not.
