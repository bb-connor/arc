\section{What is mechanized}
\label{sec:formal}

The decision core is a pure function: verify a capability, resolve grants,
evaluate, sign. It is modeled in Lean 4 \cite{lean4}, whose theorem
inventory carries \PSTheoremCount{} declarations and one explicit axiom, a
collision-resistance idealization for ASSUME-SHA256. No serializer, protocol step, or kernel behavior is axiomatized;
an unproved property is an assumption-registry entry.

Two results bear on this paper. Occurrence theorems make the predicate language
fail closed: an atom unsupported, or undefined for the admission view, anywhere
in a predicate forces denial on every view, by induction. The second is the
comparison set of Section~\ref{sec:security}: the classification of every leaf of the co-signed
predicate is total by exhaustiveness over the leaf type, and acceptance
holds exactly when the statement gate and those comparisons do.

The classification is cross-checked, not transcribed: the executed corpus
derives its enumeration from the wire type and fails unless the Lean module
agrees leaf for leaf on the wire path and the comparison kind. What each leaf is
compared against is prose on both sides, checked on neither, so a
classification naming the wrong quantity would pass.

\dfn{Lean does not verify the running Rust.} These theorems are about the
modelled predicate and classification; that the shipped hook realizes them
is a corpus over one fixture, not a theorem. Four tripwires fail
loudly when the two drift apart: named Rust functions mirrored to Lean
definitions by content hash; \PSAeneasFunctionCount{} production functions
extracted through a Rust-to-Lean pipeline with equivalence theorems that must
rebuild byte-identically; \PSKaniHarnessCount{} bounded model-checking harnesses
over public entry points; and the cross-language encoder corpus of
Section~\ref{sec:eval}.

We report survivors, not percentages. Of \PSLeanMutants{} Lean
mutants \PSLeanMutantsKilled{} were killed and \PSLeanMutantsSurvived{} survived, each
with an open issue; of \PSProofMutants{} in the Rust proof-facing sources
\PSProofMutantsKilled{} were killed, \PSProofMutantsUnviable{} were unviable,
and \PSProofMutantsSurvived{} survived.
That campaign predates the classification's entry in the mutation allowlist, so
it bears on the occurrence theorems and not the classification, and the
largest group of its survivors sits on the predicate language they are about.
