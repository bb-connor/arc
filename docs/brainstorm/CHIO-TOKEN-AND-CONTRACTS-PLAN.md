# Chio Token and Smart-Contract Roadmap (Decision)

Status: decision-oriented roadmap. Author: chief protocol architect.
Scope: whether Chio launches a native token, and how the on-chain family evolves.
Framing: fail-closed. Every escalation below is a gate that must be PROVEN open with evidence; absent proof, the gate stays closed and Chio stays at the prior phase.

## 1. Executive recommendation and the token decision gate

Recommendation: ship and stay TOKENLESS at launch (P1), but treat tokenlessness as a phased posture with hardened money-transmission defenses, not a permanent vow. Chio's five-contract family already realizes a complete settlement surface that confines value to exactly two contracts (`ChioEscrow`, `ChioBondVault`), settles in caller-supplied USDC, and exposes no mint, emission, staking, fee-accrual, or governance socket. That absence is the legal moat (SEC March-2026 digital-tool / payment-stablecoin non-security buckets, no MiCA CASP/white-paper trigger, no securities-issuance surface), and the external landscape confirms every successful agent rail (x402, Stripe MPP, Skyfire, Visa) stayed stablecoin-native. We therefore launch with USDC settlement plus a strictly closed-loop, partner-custodied prepaid credit, and we PRE-INSTRUMENT (design but do not deploy) the bond and governance seams so a future escalation ships in months, not years. The runner-up is P3's bonding mechanic denominated in USDC (voluntary self-restitution today, partner-fronted involuntary slashing later); the native `$CHIO` of P3 and the governance/value token of P4 are rejected at launch because they add securities and money-transmission surface for benefits a USDC bond or off-chain ledger already delivers.

Token decision gate (each arrow is fail-closed; do not advance until ALL exit criteria for the next phase are met and evidenced):

- Phase 0 `no-token` (LAUNCH, default): USDC-only settlement; off-chain Chio credit as a pure accounting unit. Open by default. Exit to Phase 1 requires: (a) a state-by-state MTL/MSB + GENIUS-Act memo; (b) a named licensed partner that is custodian and BSA-obligated issuer of record; (c) proof no Chio-held key (including `ChioIdentityRegistry.admin` and any settlement-key infra Chio operates) can unilaterally move escrow/bond funds.
- Phase 1 `credit` (closed-loop prepaid): ship a non-transferable, first-party-only, hard-capped prepaid credit, partner-custodied, re-socketed onto `ChioEscrow` rather than an off-TCB ledger. Gate to open: written counsel that the credit clears the FinCEN closed-loop prepaid-access exemption (31 CFR 1010.100(ff)(4)) and is not an e-money token under MiCA Art. 3(1)(7); plus a real high-frequency customer whose per-call permit/gas is a MEASURED bottleneck.
- Phase 2 `staking` (USDC bonds): turn on slashable USDC bonds for operator admission and AIUC/Lloyd's collateral. Gate to open: (a) a named insurer/surety partner that is the legal principal of record for any slash-to-third-party restitution; (b) a deployed slasher-gated bond contract with an independent adjudication/dispute/veto layer (AB-316 due-process); (c) evidence underwriters actually price premiums against a posted bond rather than receipts+tiers alone.
- Phase 3 `governance` (transferable CHIO): hand `ChioIdentityRegistry.admin` to a Governor+Timelock and, only then, consider a transferable asset. Gate to open: (a) counsel-certified, measurable decentralization of the operator set (a real "separation"), not a self-asserted precondition; (b) durable, non-artificial flat-fee volume materially above the ~$28k/day baseline; (c) no buy-and-stake / yield switch; (d) the March-2026 framework survives intact. Until all four hold, governance stays at multisig+timelock and any CHIO is soul-bound, not tradable.

## 2. Why (decisive arguments, fatal vs survivable critiques)

The decision turns on which critiques are fatal to each token thesis and which are merely survivable conditions.

Fatal to P4 (governance/value token), at launch: the Howey-at-offering circularity. P4 justifies the token as the instrument that FUNDS decentralization in a pre-revenue, founder-controlled network, yet judges classification at the moment of offering, when the network is admittedly centralized and value depends wholly on the founder's managerial efforts. The "launch only after demonstrable decentralization" precondition is circular (the token funds the decentralization it presupposes), and the buy-and-stake fee switch manufactures a managed-yield profit-expectation that the "flat-fee not take-rate" framing does not blunt. This is fatal now and survivable only in a 2027+ world with proven separation and real fees; we preserve it as Phase 3, stripped of free transferability and the yield switch.

Fatal to P3's NATIVE `$CHIO`, decisively: two independent kills. (1) Mechanism: the deterrence-or-redundancy dilemma. If `$CHIO` is minted free at registration it forfeits nothing on a Sybil re-spawn (theater); if it must be bought with USDC it is a cosmetic wrapper around USDC that adds only classification surface. A non-priced soul-bound unit also cannot back AIUC/Lloyd's premiums, which are marked in dollars. (2) Technical: confirmed against bytecode, `ChioBondVault.impairBondDetailed` (contracts/src/ChioBondVault.sol:134) requires `msg.sender == bond.terms.operator` and (`:136`) verifies the evidence root via `rootRegistry.verifyInclusionDetailed(..., bond.terms.operator)` against a root the SAME operator published. The existing "slash socket" is voluntary self-forfeiture, NOT slashing-against-will; a relayer's `msg.sender` is not the operator and reverts `UnauthorizedCaller`. So "the slash socket exists, zero new value contracts" is false for involuntary slashing. SURVIVING idea: the bonding mechanic in USDC (self-restitution today; a NEW slasher-gated vault later) is genuinely valuable and is grafted as Phase 2.

Fatal to P2's on-chain restricted-ERC20 SETTLEMENT leg: three kills. (1) Legal: a par-pegged, fully reserved, redeemable-1:1 instrument is the textbook MiCA e-money token (Art. 3(1)(7)), issuable only by an authorized EMI, not a utility token; and money-transmission liability attaches to ISSUANCE/REDEMPTION, which a partner custodying float does not cure unless the partner is issuer of record. (2) Technical fund-stranding: `ChioEscrow.sol:264` does `_transferToken(terms.token, escrow.terms.beneficiary, amount)` to a dynamic non-sink operator, and `ChioBondVault.sol:156` transfers slash shares to arbitrary beneficiaries; a restricted allowlist either reverts (stranding funds inside immutable contracts, the position's own kill-criterion) or pays the provider unredeemable credit. (3) Unit-of-account: `chio-underwriting/premium.rs:141` rejects any currency where `len() != 3` or not all-uppercase, so "CHIOCREDIT" breaks premium pricing immediately. SURVIVING ideas: the single-denomination netting collapse and an on-chain terminal state for the IOU lifecycle - both achievable off-chain in `chio-credit`'s ExposureLedger, or by re-socketing the prepaid balance onto `ChioEscrow`. These are grafted as Phase 1.

Survivable-but-load-bearing critiques of P1 (which is why P1 wins WITH fixes, not as written): (1) Money transmission, not securities, is the real flank. P1's claim that refusing a bps take-rate is "the single most license-triggering choice" is legally false; MSB/MTL status turns on accept-and-transmit or hold-value, not on flat-vs-bps pricing. That prose must be struck and the defense re-grounded on the FinCEN 2019 CVC non-custodial-software exemption plus agent-of-the-payee. (2) The prepaid-float pillar is mis-socketed: `IouEnvelopeBody` (chio-credit/src/hook.rs:57) is a POST-consumption obligation minted only when `cost_charged > 0`; a prepayment has no receipt to mint from, so the float needs a seam that does not exist and would live outside the kernel-signed, offline-verifiable chain. Fix: re-socket prepaid balances onto `ChioEscrow` (a pre-funded deposit with refund-after-deadline as the no-cashout lever) so they stay inside the verifiable chain, or explicitly carve them out of the verifiability claim. (3) The "structural prevention" claim for not pinning CHIO in `minor_units_for_currency` overstates: it fails closed only at the conversion boundary (`chio-link/src/convert.rs`), since `MonetaryAmount.currency` is a free-form String; state it as fail-closed-at-conversion. (4) The `ChioIdentityRegistry` advisory stablecoin allowlist is inert because the immutable money contracts never read the identity registry for token approval; keep it only as operator tooling metadata, not a settlement gate. None of these is fatal; each is a fixable over-claim. With them fixed, P1 carries strictly the lowest aggregate regulatory liability (securities + MiCA + money-transmission) and the lowest integration cost (zero new immutable contracts, four contracts untouched).

Net: tokenless is the lowest-liability, fastest-to-ship, neutrality-preserving posture in a thin/artificial ~$28k/day market, and the surviving token ideas are exactly the phased escalations the gate already encodes.

## 3. Smart-contract roadmap

Constraints honored throughout: four value contracts (`ChioRootRegistry`, `ChioEscrow`, `ChioBondVault`, `ChioPriceResolver`) are immutable; only `ChioIdentityRegistry` is mutable (single `admin` via `transferAdmin`, ChioIdentityRegistry.sol:32). The money boundary stays narrow: value transfer is confined to `ChioEscrow` and `ChioBondVault`. New economic behavior arrives as NEW deployments alongside the immutable spine, never as edits to it.

Phase 0 (launch, no new contracts):
- `ChioEscrow` / `ChioBondVault`: zero change. `terms.token` stays an arbitrary IERC20 operationally constrained to a curated stablecoin set (USDC/USDT) by off-chain policy.
- `ChioPriceResolver`: register only USDC<>fiat and stablecoin<>stablecoin Chainlink feeds via admin `registerFeed`, retaining staleness + L2 sequencer-up checks. Do NOT register a CHIO/USD feed (no CHIO exists).
- `ChioRootRegistry`: unchanged; remains the compliance-export backbone (`publishRoot`/`publishRootBatch`, `verifyInclusionDetailed`).
- `ChioIdentityRegistry`: keep `admin` as multisig+timelock. Optionally record an advisory, non-gating `isApprovedSettlementToken` allowlist as operator tooling metadata only. Document explicitly that this CANNOT fail-close settlement because the immutable money contracts do not read it.

Phase 1 (credit) - NO new immutable money contract; re-socket onto escrow:
- Prepaid credit = a pre-funded `ChioEscrow` position (`createEscrow` / `createEscrowWithPermit`, `terms.token = USDC`) named to the licensed credit-service operator, with `refund` after `deadline` as the no-cashout / non-transferability lever. The off-chain service tracks decrements against a kernel-verifiable escrow position; it never custodies an unbacked balance. This keeps the credit inside the published-root, offline-verifiable chain and needs zero new deployments.
- Do NOT build the P2 restricted-ERC20 `ChioCreditVault`. It either strands funds against `ChioEscrow.sol:264` / `ChioBondVault.sol:156` or pays providers unredeemable credit, and it imports e-money classification.

Phase 2 (staking) - bonding in USDC; one NEW immutable contract only if involuntary slashing is required:
- Voluntary self-restitution (available now, zero new contract): operators post USDC into `ChioBondVault.lockBond` (`terms.token = USDC`) and self-execute `impairBondDetailed` to pay harmed `ChioEscrow` depositors, a credible-commitment signal underwriters can price. This works within the existing self-slash authorization (ChioBondVault.sol:134).
- Involuntary, federation-gated slashing (NEW contract `ChioSlashableBondVault`): because the immutable `ChioBondVault` only lets the operator slash itself, enforceable slashing requires a new immutable vault whose impair function is authorized to a registry/committee-gated slasher role (admin = multisig+timelock; corroboration = `FederatedSybilControl` minimum_independent_issuers >= 2) and that accepts a root anchored by an incident registry rather than the offender's own tree. This is a net-new value contract in the TCB requiring fresh audit and an explicit change to the custody story; it ships ONLY behind the Phase 2 gate and a licensed partner as principal of record. Restitution to third-party buyers is paid under the partner's insurance/surety license, not directly via a Chio `impairBondDetailed` beneficiaries array.
- Restricted-transfer `$CHIO` ERC-20: NOT built. It is a new value-bearing contract in the custody TCB that buys nothing over a USDC bond.

Phase 3 (governance) - only after the Phase 3 gate:
- `ChioIdentityRegistry.transferAdmin(timelock)` hands `registerOperator` / `deactivateOperator` / `registerDelegate` to a NEW `ChioGovernor` + `TimelockController`. This is a literal zero-Solidity-edit handoff on the one mutable contract.
- NEW contracts only if and when a token is justified: `ChioTreasury` / `FeeRouter` (receives flat `OpenMarketFeeScheduleArtifact` fees and `OpenMarketBondRequirement` amounts in USDC), and, if a token is approved, a soul-bound governance/bonding credential first. No buy-and-stake, no yield switch, no `ChioPriceResolver` CHIO/USD feed unless a transferable asset clears the gate. The registry sits behind a reputation-gated proposer set plus a veto committee so a thin-market majority cannot directly admit malicious operators.

## 4. Off-chain / crate roadmap

Phase 0 (launch):
- `chio-credit`: keep `LocalCreditAccount` minting `IouEnvelopeBody` only from non-zero `financial.cost_charged`, currency pinned to USD/USDC; hold `cross_currency_netting_supported = false` (lib.rs:237) and `capital_allocation_supported = false`; keep books `mixed_currency_book`. Per-currency fail-closed exposure accounting is the prudential safeguard.
- `chio-link`: keep `minor_units_for_currency` pinning only USD/EUR/GBP/JPY/USDC/USDT/BTC/ETH/LINK and failing closed (`InvalidConfiguration`) on any unpinned code; do NOT pin CHIO. State this as fail-closed-at-conversion, not "structural prevention."
- `chio-metering`: `CostMetadata.total_monetary_cost` and `CostDimension::ApiCost` stay fiat `MonetaryAmount`; `compute_total_monetary_cost` keeps dropping cross-currency amounts and deferring to the oracle.
- `chio-settle`: `ApprovalBinding.{token_symbol, token_contract}` and X402/Circle lanes keep asserting USDC via `assert_token`; this fiat-string-to-on-chain-token bridge resolves only to a stablecoin contract.
- `chio-web3-bindings`: `IChioEscrow` / `IChioBondVault` / `IChioPriceResolver` bindings unchanged.
- Trust crates: `chio-federation` keeps `FederatedSybilControl` (min_independent_issuers=2, oracle_cap_bps=4000) attestation-based; `chio-pheromone` keeps `PheromoneScarcityPolicy.token_capacity` as a per-window rate-limit quota; `chio-reputation` keeps penalties as score reductions (`DEFAULT_INCIDENT_PENALTY = 0.20`) and tier gating (`TIER_3_PER_FEED_THRESHOLD`), preserving receipts-not-stake Sybil resistance.

Phase 1 (credit):
- `chio-credit`: add a prepaid-balance view that decrements against a kernel-verifiable `ChioEscrow` position; do NOT add an off-TCB ledger. If a single-denomination accounting benefit is wanted, collapse the ExposureLedger to one USDC-canonical code OFF-CHAIN (this delivers the netting/capital-allocation win the token theses claimed, with no on-chain instrument). Keep `cross_currency_netting_supported` switchable per credit-denominated book only after the off-chain collapse is proven.
- Currency code hygiene: if any non-USD code is ever introduced for credits, it MUST be a 3-letter uppercase ISO private-use code (for example "XCC") pinned in `minor_units_for_currency`, never a 10-char string, because `chio-underwriting/premium.rs:141` fail-closed rejects non-3-letter codes.

Phase 2 (staking):
- `chio-reputation`: extend `incident_correlation` so an `IncidentRecord` crossing severity emits a signed `SlashInstruction` artifact (amount by incident class) in ADDITION to the score delta. Stake must NEVER raise `peer_weight`, `weight_bps`, or lower the tier-3 floor; capital buys the right to be slashed, not trust.
- `chio-federation`: gate any slash on `FederatedReputationInputKind::NegativeEvent` with `blocking=true` and corroboration (min_independent_issuers=2, oracle_cap_bps=4000) so no single issuer can slash unilaterally.
- `chio-revocation-oracle`: attach the `SlashInstruction` to `SignedEpochRoot` gossip for eventually-consistent slash-then-revoke (NOT atomic; an on-chain tx and an off-chain cache merge share no commit primitive - drop any "atomic slash+revoke" claim).
- `chio-underwriting`: add the restitution/insurance pool as a `CapitalBookSourceKind::ReserveBook` destination ONLY on a licensed partner's books; add a `bond_depth` input to `price_premium` so larger live USDC bonds lower `base_rate_cents`. Bonds are denominated in USDC so underwriters can mark to market.
- `chio-pheromone` / `chio-open-market`: make `token_capacity` and `OpenMarketBondRequirement.required_amount` resolvable against a live USDC bond tier (binary admission gate), without folding stake into trust weight.
- New prepare-only slash relayer in `chio-web3` style: simulation-first, custody-neutral, executed only by the licensed partner signer.

Phase 3 (governance):
- `chio-governance`: bind on-chain Governor outcomes to issuance of `SignedGenericGovernanceCharter` via the operator/human multisig that already holds the kernel Ed25519 key (a `TimelockController` cannot sign canonical JSON). Governor is advisory input; the existing trusted signer remains the principal. Keep stake out of the deterministic reputation computation entirely.
- `chio-open-market`: route flat fees to the governed `ChioTreasury`; keep fees flat, never bps.

## 5. Research plan (resolve before escalating)

Each item lists owner-type and exit criterion (fail-closed: unresolved = gate stays closed).

1. Money-transmission / MSB / MTL memo (owner: outside fintech regulatory counsel). Exit: written 50-state MTL/MSB + FinCEN CVC + GENIUS-Act analysis confirming (a) escrow/bond are non-custodial under the FinCEN 2019 exemption (no Chio-held key moves user value), and (b) the prepaid credit is either closed-loop-exempt or sits on a licensed partner as issuer of record. Gates Phase 0->1.
2. Non-custody proof of the escrow/bond key surface (owner: protocol security lead). Exit: a documented demonstration that no Chio-controlled key, including `ChioIdentityRegistry.admin` and settlement-key infra, can unilaterally release or redirect escrowed funds. Gates Phase 0->1.
3. MiCA e-money classification opinion (owner: EU counsel). Exit: opinion that the Phase 1 credit is NOT an e-money token under Art. 3(1)(7), or, if it is, a named EMI as issuer of record. Gates Phase 1.
4. Closed-loop prepaid-access structuring (owner: product + counsel). Exit: per-user caps, first-party-only redemption, no operator-to-operator redemption, technically (not policy-only) enforced non-transferability, signed off as within 31 CFR 1010.100(ff)(4). Gates Phase 1.
5. Insurer/underwriter validation (owner: BD + actuarial partner; AIUC / Lloyd's / Nexus). Exit: written confirmation whether underwriters price premiums off a POSTED bond vs receipts+tiers alone, and whether a USDC bond suffices. If receipts+tiers suffice, Phase 2 bonding is deferred. Gates Phase 2.
6. Slash-adjudication and AB-316 due-process design (owner: mechanism-design + counsel). Exit: a dispute window, independent adjudicator, and EigenLayer-style veto committee specified, with a legal opinion that the licensed partner (not Chio) is transmitter and insurer of record for any slash-to-third-party flow. Gates Phase 2.
7. Mechanism-design modeling of bond sizing (owner: mechanism-design). Exit: a model showing bond >= per-session max-extractable-value for the capabilities that matter, with low floors for observation-only scopes to preserve onboarding. Gates Phase 2.
8. Decentralization measurement and securities opinion (owner: counsel + protocol). Exit: an objective, on-chain-measurable decentralization metric (independent-operator threshold, foundation-stake ceiling, Nakamoto-style coefficient) and counsel certification that a transferable CHIO sits in the digital-commodity bucket. Gates Phase 3.

M3 (self-restitution) is independent of M2 and can run in parallel. M4 and beyond are partner-gated and must not start build until the partner is contractually committed.

## 7. Risks and kill-criteria

Abandon the token program entirely (collapse permanently to Phase 0/1) if any of these hold:

- Demand stays thin and partly artificial (around $28k/day, ~50% mirage) through 2027. No fee base means any token is narrative, not market; defer indefinitely. (Kills Phase 2 economic rationale and Phase 3.)
- No licensed partner will be issuer/custodian/insurer of record. Then issuing a credit or running a restitution pool forces Chio to custody value or pay third-party claims, breaking the custody-neutral spine and triggering money-transmission / unlicensed-insurance exposure. Kill the credit program (Phase 1) and involuntary slashing (Phase 2) before that line is crossed.
- Off-chain netting (M1) is proven to fully deliver the single-denomination benefit. Then any on-chain credit token is pure surface area; never build it.
- Underwriters price off receipts + reputation tiers WITHOUT a posted bond. Then the bond is friction with no premium payoff; keep reputation-only and drop Phase 2 bonding.
- Counsel cannot keep a would-be token non-transferable and non-redeemable in practice (secondary market, OTC redemption, or yield emerges). Then the digital-tool / non-security classification breaks and MiCA CASP duties trigger; abandon the token.
- A USDC/ETH bond into a slasher-gated vault delivers equal enforcement and insurance utility. Then the native `$CHIO` is cosmetic; keep the mechanic, never mint the asset (EigenLayer / Nexus lesson).
- Decentralization is unverifiable or gameable (founder distributes to friendly wallets while retaining real control). Then "separation" fails, CHIO reads as a security; do not ship a transferable token.
- The SEC March-2026 framework is rescinded or narrowed, or no real safe harbor emerges. Then the entire token-classification posture loses its footing; fall back to Phase 0.
- The closed-loop credit cannot be technically locked to first-party-only redemption. Kill the credit program (not the no-token stance) before non-transferability fails in practice.

## 8. Appendix: fair summary of the four positions and placement

P1 (Tokenless: stablecoin-native settlement, Chio credit as off-chain accounting). PRIMARY (with fixes). The strongest position on securities/MiCA (no transferable instrument, no profit marketing, no issuance trigger) and the lowest-integration option (zero new immutable contracts, four contracts untouched, only the already-mutable `ChioIdentityRegistry` touched and only for inert metadata). It placed first because the decisive market and legal signals (every comparable rail tokenless; thin/artificial demand; money-transmission as the dominant constraint) all favor it. It did NOT place first as written: it under-defends the money-transmission flank (the flat-vs-bps claim is legally irrelevant and must be struck), mis-sockets the prepaid-float pillar onto the post-consumption `IouEnvelopeBody` path that cannot mint a prepayment, and over-claims "structural prevention" for the no-CHIO pin (it is only fail-closed-at-conversion). Those are survivable, so P1 wins on a corrected version of its own reasoning.

P3 (Staking + Bonding + Slashing: native `$CHIO`). RUNNER-UP, but only as the USDC-bond mechanic, not the native asset. Its real insight is correct and important: today's penalties (reputation score reduction, blocking event, revocation) are non-economic, so a misbehaving provider's worst case is a free Sybil re-spawn; a slashable bond imposes a sunk cost. It placed second because that mechanic is the most likely first escalation (insurer-demanded collateral) and maps onto real seams. The NATIVE token is rejected: it faces a deterrence-or-redundancy dilemma (free-minted is theater, USDC-bought is cosmetic), cannot be marked to market by underwriters, and adds securities surface for no benefit. Its "zero new value contracts / slash socket exists" claim is fatally false against `ChioBondVault.sol:134-136`, which permits only operator self-slash; involuntary slashing needs a new audited vault and a licensed partner. We graft the mechanic (USDC bonds, self-restitution now, partner-fronted involuntary slashing later) as Phase 2.

P2 (ChioCredit: restricted USDC-denominated prepaid credit). THIRD; the on-chain instrument is rejected, its accounting benefit is grafted. It correctly identifies the one genuinely missing money-seam (no on-chain metering/credit contract) and the single-denomination netting prize. But the on-chain restricted-ERC20 is fatally mis-fit: it is a MiCA e-money token by construction (par peg + reserve + redemption), it strands funds against the immutable escrow/bond transfer-to-non-sink paths (`ChioEscrow.sol:264`, `ChioBondVault.sol:156`), and its proposed "CHIOCREDIT" code breaks `chio-underwriting/premium.rs:141`. Its own cons concede the netting benefit is realizable off-chain. So we take the surviving idea (single-denomination collapse + an escrow-socketed prepaid balance) into Phase 1 and discard the new contract.

P4 (CHIO: governance + value-accrual network token). FOURTH; deferred to a conditional terminal phase. It correctly locates the only real governance primitive (`ChioIdentityRegistry.transferAdmin`, a zero-edit handoff) and a real flat-fee base (`OpenMarketFeeScheduleArtifact`). It placed last because it is the highest-liability thesis: an unavoidable, self-contradictory Howey-at-offering problem (the token funds the decentralization it claims to presuppose), a buy-and-stake yield switch that manufactures profit-expectation, free transferability plus a CHIO/USD feed that invites narrative pricing in a thin market, and a forfeiture of the neutrality wedge that lets Chio ride incumbent rails. Its survivable core (governance of the real registry primitive via Governor+Timelock, flat fees to a treasury) is preserved as Phase 3, stripped of free transferability and the yield switch, and gated behind counsel-certified decentralization and real fee volume that do not exist today.
