## Obstacle, mechanism, established result, consequence

**Obstacle.** Useful autonomous work crosses owners of data, tools, budgets and
money. A timeout does not reveal whether an external effect occurred; a refund
does not erase that effect; an authenticated message does not activate a
receiver's authority. A system must retain those distinctions while permitting
independent authorized work. These are consequential engineering constraints,
but identifying them does not by itself establish a new research problem or
solution.

**Mechanism.** Chio joins original operation custody, owner-scoped authorization,
materialized inputs, typed prerequisites, retained knowledge, bounded resources
and independently backed obligations at mediated work boundaries. For this
research, the complete recovery specifications at PR #1172 revision
`de84fc306efbb4c8dd6de748d0ad2a8d695fd30e` are assumed shipped as instructed.
The candidate exposes named obligations; B1 implements equally provisioned
conventional guards. Both consume the same recovery semantics. Their comparable
outcomes matter more than their different presentation.

**Established result.** Under the declared qualified-host/provider, authentic
approval, durable-owner, classifier and settlement premises, KW1 passes the
finite directed and bounded-exploration checks described in [G2](results/G2.md),
with no candidate/B1 difference. The same core supports 28 paired synthetic
cases across two families, with shared adapter logic fully counted. At the
actual pinned native source, real process deaths across 10 local-owner
trajectories preserve original request/nonce custody, prevent kernel redispatch
of unknown work and permit other owners to complete. A deliberately retrying
connector nevertheless produces two physical effects in one invocation. The
native result therefore requires explicit transport qualification and does not
establish full KW1 refinement, universal confinement or independent operation.

**Consequence.** There is an executable and reviewable contract experiment,
useful native regression coverage and a much more precise empirical study.
There is no established new theorem, capability unavailable to the strongest
baseline, integration-cost advantage or useful-economic result. DLM/DStar cover
owner-based information flow, recoverable RPC constructions cover retained
operation outcomes, and interface contracts/knowledge-based control cover much
of the composition and enabled-action reasoning. B1 is the concrete check that
these techniques can be provisioned comparably here. The
[source-audited comparison](counterdesigns.md) and [witness](WITNESS.md) remain
the basis for that judgment; these experiments do not establish a contrary
literature-wide novelty claim.

## Strongest objection and what would change this decision

The strongest objection is that the candidate reorganizes established recovery,
capability, information-flow and escrow techniques into a coherent kernel, while
a competent composition has the same useful behavior. Our own provisioned B1
supports that objection. Equivalence does not disprove the value of a unified
system; it makes measured integration or performance consequences essential to
the present research route. Describing the system as an economic kernel cannot
substitute for those consequences.

A strong systems result could be established by closing the native composition
crosswalk and meeting the preregistered independent integration threshold with
matched safety, quality, progress and complete cost accounting. That would
change the judgment to a supported, consequential systems contribution. It
would not automatically establish a foundational mechanism breakthrough.

A foundational claim needs a precisely stated new mechanism, theorem or
tradeoff that remains nontrivial when B1 receives the same primitives and useful
baseline improvements. Any renewed search must begin with a falsifiable witness
and prior-art comparison before implementation. Reintroducing the already
rejected unconditional exactly-once or exclusive-safe-progress claims would not
change this decision.
