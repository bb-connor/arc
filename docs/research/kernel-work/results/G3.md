## Results and adapter accounting

Of 28 paired cases, 17 refuse in the shared adapter before KW1. The other 11
enter the model in each arm. Authorized support publication and research return
complete. A lost ACK retains an actual effect and withholds release. Revoked
audience prevents delivery. Missing owner or wrong authority class prevents the
effect; a denied research seed prevents downstream work. Changed payload,
version, recipient and output channel refuse. Research extra fields, out-of-range
evidence and oversized returns refuse even when their exact values have approvals.

Three additional test functions cover rebinding family identity, omitted seeds,
malformed payloads and positive/negative companions. These are direct assertions
as well as parity checks; parity alone would miss shared adapter errors.

Every added adapter obligation is charged equally to both arms:

| Added correctness work | Why it is needed | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full materialization/approval equality | Prevent reduction from dropping family, payload, version, channel or recipient binding | Unsigned genuine-approval premise; fixed symbolic principals |
| Support and return schemas | Make the approved issue concrete and the child return bounded | Synthetic schemas, no semantic classifier or task-quality proof |
| Qualified-host and seed requirements | Refuse a research path without its declared confinement premise | Boolean premise, not attestation or sandbox enforcement |
| Seed refusal stops continuation | A refused seed cannot still produce downstream effects | Shared host-side scheduling rule, counted as adapter logic |
| Read-result decision gates release | Unknown outcome or revoked audience cannot release the modeled result | Shared pipeline rule, not universal channel mediation |
| Per-materialization symbol mapping | Reuse KW1 recipient/version symbols after exact validation | Fresh namespace per run; no multi-artifact native refinement |

There are no candidate-only policy exceptions or unreported host repairs. No
measured implementation-cost inference follows from this inventory. B1 gets the
same reusable adapter, so these results supply no observed comparative advantage.
