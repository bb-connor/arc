# G0 decision: pursue one composition question

Date: 2026-10-02. Decision scope: Task 1 of the
[approved research plan](../../superpowers/plans/2026-10-02-kernel-breakthrough-research.md).

**Proceed to Task 2 with a narrowed R1 systems question.** B1, B2 and B3 are
credible constructions for the target behavior. There is no established
exclusive Chio capability, new algorithm, equivalence proof or breakthrough.
The first task succeeds by making those alternatives and the remaining question
precise enough to test. It does not require a favorable novelty verdict.

## The single retained question

> Can the complete Chio kernel/recovery architecture provide a reusable contract
> between independently owned work participants that preserves exact authority,
> knowledge restrictions, resource exposure and earned obligations through
> failure, with a nontrivial composition argument and a consequential reduction
> in repeated correctness machinery or coordination?

The difficult case remains the paid specialist followed by exact multi-owner
publication, lost acknowledgement and parent cancellation. No extra invention
unrelated to the shipped recovery specification is required. The architecture
itself may contain the result, if its contract and advantage survive comparison.

The observable difference must be one of the following, established under
matched assumptions rather than presumed now:

1. A nontrivial local-to-global sufficiency result for J1-J4, with a concrete
   counterexample when an essential premise is removed and a substantive
   distinction from existing capability/IFC/recovery composition.
2. A specific continuation procedure or representation that changes useful
   progress, required coordination or state cost on the shared finite profile.
3. A reusable systems interface that preserves the same guarantees while
   substantially reducing repeated integration and repair across task families.

The third route already has a proposed empirical bar in the original program:
median repeated integration time at most half the matched alternative across
six paired exercises, and at least 95% of scheduled recoverable incidents
resolved without database edits or bespoke repair. Those are thresholds, not
results. A speedup alone would still need a scientific significance judgment
before becoming the flagship thesis.

## What the strongest alternatives changed

The [counterdesigns](counterdesigns.md) specify records, authority, store commit
boundaries, label handling, funding attachment, effect adapters and a worked
trajectory for each construction. A builder may improve them, add fields,
combine stores, use independent local engines and amortize shared libraries.

- **B1 is the primary falsifier.** Durable independent services, owner-qualified
  flow policy and ordinary escrow have a plausible path to every required
  behavior. The remaining question is compositional sufficiency and cost.
- **B2 is the capability/economy control.** Durable objects and safe allocations
  are strong existing foundations; external effects and information-flow hosts
  need explicit integration and trust. The comparison matches that integration.
- **B3 is the recovery/progress control.** OpenAPPA already has meaningful local
  remedy enumeration, durable claims and uncertain-effect reservations. Give its
  host the same provider and owner-policy semantics before looking for a gap.

The source pass also found direct older antecedents for decentralized owner
labels and cross-host enforcement (DLM/DStar), and inspected established
observation-based control. Recent agent papers already claim contracts,
multiple governing authorities and mandatory kernel mediation. Packaging those
concepts under one title is insufficient differentiation. These are source-backed
overlaps; the complete new counterdesigns have not yet been proved equivalent.

## Claim dispositions

| Claim | G0 disposition | What could defeat it next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 composition | Retain as the primary systems question; exact records in the [claim register](claim-register.json) | A composition counterexample, or equal behavior/cost from a reusable baseline with no nontrivial new result |
| R2 continuation | Retain only as R1's first probe; no separate new-algorithm claim | B1/B3 obtains the same safe continuations with comparable representation and coordination; a restatement of known partial-observation reasoning |
| R3 integration | Retain as a separate empirical route; no automatic substitute for the requested foundational result | Matched shared libraries and independent teams erase the effort/recovery advantage |
| Receiver-owned authority exclusivity | Reject as unsupported, preserving the earlier counterexample | Ordinary local authority and token/profile extensions are allowed |
| Escrow/child independence as breakthrough | Reject, preserving the ERC-8183 negative result | Independently backed escrow already supplies the property |
| Local recovery, all-owner disclosure or signed uncertainty as novelty | Reject as standalone claims | OpenAPPA, DLM/DStar and ordinary durable protocols already overlap |
| Universal recovery or secret protection from a malicious authorized recipient | Reject | Identical E2 observations cannot reveal hidden effects; a plaintext recipient can disclose what it legitimately learns |

These are dispositions of proposed headlines, not a claim that Chio lacks value.
The actual question is whether a compact common kernel interface removes a
major correctness burden in an independently operated work economy.

## Next tasks and stop conditions

**Task 2: one shared model and F01-F16.** Define domain ownership, separate
execution/knowledge/financial state, exact edge assumptions, observation classes,
validity lifetimes and E1/E2 provider contracts. Write the B1 and Chio transitions
against the same contract. Include allowed approved work, authoritative lookup
and independent sibling progress alongside every refusal. Produce `MODEL.md`
and `fixtures.json`. Do not start by implementing another runtime.

**Task 3: one minimal witness and G1.** Try first to translate the difficult
trajectory into B1 without weakening any premise. Identify the smallest place
where the proposed contract changes proof burden, state, coordination or
repeated integration. Produce `WITNESS.md` and `G1-DECISION.md`. If there is no
surviving mechanism difference, record that result and decide whether a
preregistered consequential systems comparison warrants proceeding. Do not
create a larger subsystem merely to defer an equivalence finding.

**Task 4, conditional on G1:** build only the bounded model/executable comparison
and proofs justified by that decision. Native source correspondence is Task 5;
second-family and independent comparative evidence are Task 6. Re-enter the
manuscript only at G4. An impressive abstract cannot substitute for that result.
