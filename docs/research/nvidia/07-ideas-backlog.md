# Ideas backlog

This backlog lists every idea considered for placing Chio relative to NVIDIA's agent safety stack: the OpenShell runtime, the Sentry reference design, the Secure Agent Workspace (SAW) reference design, and the Open Delegation & Identity Standard (ODIS) draft. There is one table per category. Each row gives the idea, why it matters, the evidence, a size, an impact, its dependencies, and a status with the reason. Rejected ideas stay in the tables with their reasons, so that they are reopened only on new evidence.

The NVIDIA side is described in [01-nvidia-stack.md](01-nvidia-stack.md), Chio's current state in [02-chio-today.md](02-chio-today.md), the capability comparison in [03-overlap-matrix.md](03-overlap-matrix.md), the integration mechanisms in [04-integration-design.md](04-integration-design.md), competitors in [05-competitive-analysis.md](05-competitive-analysis.md), and sequencing in [06-strategy-and-roadmap.md](06-strategy-and-roadmap.md). Sources are collected in [08-sources.md](08-sources.md).

## How to read the tables

### Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | Stable identifier, I-001 to I-168. Other documents in this set refer to ideas by ID. |
| Idea | What would be built, changed, said, or decided. Every idea is a proposal: none is built, and none is on the repository roadmap until an ADR or design document adopts it. |
| Rationale | Why the idea matters, with a label on each factual statement. |
| Evidence | The sources the rationale rests on. |
| Effort | Estimated size, defined below. |
| Impact | Estimated effect on Chio's position, defined below. |
| Depends on | Ideas that must land first. "None" means the idea can start now. |
| Status and reason | One of recommended, optional, later, or rejected, and why. |

### Status values

- **recommended**: part of the plan. [06-strategy-and-roadmap.md](06-strategy-and-roadmap.md) sequences it.
- **optional**: worth doing when a design partner asks or capacity frees up.
- **later**: after the first 90 days, or blocked on an outside event.
- **rejected**: do not do. The reason records what would have to change.

### Effort, impact, and time

- Effort: XS is a decision, a question, or a wording change. S is up to about 2 engineer-weeks, M about 2 to 6, and L about 2 months or more. A range such as S-M spans both sizes. Where the estimate is in weeks, the row shows it. All sizes are estimates (INFERRED).
- Impact runs High, Medium-High, Medium, Low-Medium, Low. Negative means the idea would weaken Chio's position. None, Unknown, Speculative, and Potentially High mark ideas the evidence cannot rate. Impact is our assessment (INFERRED).
- "Day 30" and "days 31 to 60" count from the start of the 90-day plan in [06-strategy-and-roadmap.md](06-strategy-and-roadmap.md). "Q2" means the second 90-day period of that plan, not a calendar quarter.
- A "wedge" is a narrow first offering that gets Chio into an account. "The first wedge" is the single-organization offering at OpenShell's chokepoints: argument checks, receipts, and the evidence built on them.
