### Relation labels

- **Chio superset**: Chio covers what the NVIDIA side defines on this dimension and more.
- **NVIDIA superset**: the NVIDIA side covers what Chio defines and more.
- **Equivalent**: both define the same function at comparable strength.
- **Complementary**: both address the dimension, at different layers or over different objects. Neither replaces the other.
- **Absent in Chio**, **Absent in NVIDIA**, **Absent in both**: no definition exists on that side.

Relations compare what each side defines, whether built or only specified. On the Chio side that means `main`, including its design documents; branch-only work appears in cells but does not change a relation. Whether a definition is built is carried by the status label in each cell. Every relation label is our assessment (INFERRED) from the evidence listed for its row.

## 1. OpenShell policy domains vs Chio capability scopes and guards

An OpenShell 0.1.x policy (`version: 1`) has five sections. `filesystem_policy`, `landlock`, and `process` take effect at sandbox start; `network_policies` and `network_middlewares` change live. There is no inference section (VERIFIED). Chio authority is a capability scope of tool, resource, and prompt grants with typed constraints. The kernel checks it on each mediated call, together with a guard pipeline compiled from HushSpec, a YAML policy format (shipped). OpenShell decides what a process may touch, and Chio decides which calls a signed authority chain permits (INFERRED).

## 2. Delegation record: SAW and ODIS vs Chio capability token and delegation link

SAW describes a signed per-engagement delegation record that binds four identities to a task, scoped resources, tools, duration, approval mode, revocation, and audit references. It is "enforced at every tool call by the runtime layer, revocable by the sponsor", and "A child delegation record can only narrow its parent's scope, tools, resources, and duration" (PROPOSED). SAW specifies no wire format, signature algorithm, issuer key, owning component, or maximum lifetime, and it describes identity and delegation as "consumed from enterprise IAM / ODIS rather than owned" (VERIFIED). ODIS section 6.3 defines a carrier-neutral Delegation Record with 13 minimum MUST fields plus `parent_delegation_ref` for non-root records (PROPOSED). Chio's closest artifact is `chio.capability.v1` with its chain of signed `DelegationLink` entries (qualified).

## 5. Sentry and DOCA attested telemetry vs sensor-grounded admission
