On Linux where PID 1 does not promptly reap orphaned children, `process.poll()` reaps only the direct wrapper; after the group is killed, its descendant can remain as a zombie, so `killpg(pgid, 0)` continues to report the group as present through the one-second grace period. Running the checked-in `distribution-review-regressions.py` in this Linux environment leaves such zombies and fails both descendant-cleanup tests, so the exact implementation snippet cannot reliably reach the documented green state; use a subreaper/cgroup wait or an observation that distinguishes terminated zombies from live descendants.
