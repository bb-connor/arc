**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind each ToolResult to the invocation**

`lower_bound` verifies only that the verdict matches `InvocationBinding`; `ToolResult` remains unbound bytes. With concurrent calls, passing invocation/verdict B together with result A therefore passes every proposed check, applies B's redactions to A's output, and labels the response with B's correlation. This preserves the cross-call output leak the design is intended to remove. Carry a trusted invocation digest or receipt binding alongside `ToolResult` and verify it before lowering, without parsing correlation from the output bytes themselves.
