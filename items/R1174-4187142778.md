**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Exclude reusable withheld receipts from the final set**

Fresh evidence after expanding `final(r)` to count successful outcomes is that it now counts every `Withheld` decision, even though the text immediately above and spec 9 M20 explicitly classify a check-only read's `Withheld { retry: AfterResume }` receipt as `Reusable`. After the stop clears, the same replay identity may legally produce an `Allow`; this formula then places both receipts in `terminal_receipts` and falsely violates the at-most-one invariant. Count `Withheld` only when its identity disposition is `Terminal`, leaving reusable withheld attempts outside the final set.
