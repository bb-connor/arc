**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Use the authority clock for process caller checks**

When the kernel uses a fixed or externally synchronized authority clock that differs from the host wall clock, this second expiry check can reject a `ToolInvocationContext` that the kernel has just admitted. `submit_child`, child-wait recording, and attested mailbox send/claim/complete operations all call `ProcessRegistry::caller`, so valid process workflows fail even though capability admission succeeded; sample the kernel authority clock here (or rely on the admitted context) instead of `SystemTime`.
