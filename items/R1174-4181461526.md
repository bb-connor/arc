**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Give executors the complete v2 binding digest**

For an `Allow` whose output was not observed by the kernel, this tells the executor to stamp the digest from the `ToolInvocation` it executed. When a delegation-permit or model-context digest is present, however, the v2 binding digest also includes those values and cannot be reconstructed from `ToolInvocation`; stamping its invocation digest makes `lower_bound` reject an otherwise valid caller-executed result. Pass the complete `InvocationBinding` or the sealed record's binding digest to the executor for this path.
