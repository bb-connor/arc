**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Preserve every pending transition for a scope**

The new per-scope merge fixes cross-scope overwrites, but it still cannot represent two uncommitted transitions for the same scope. For example, if a `Stop` at epoch 5 is latch-only and a narrower `Restrict` is recorded as epoch 6 before recovery, this merge retains only one `transition`; after restart the durable head is still running at epoch 4, so appending the retained `Restrict` violates S2, while appending one `Stop` at epoch 5 cannot clear an entry whose `intended_epoch` is 6. Keep an ordered per-scope transition queue, or collapse pending requests into one next-epoch `Stop` whose payload represents the final restrictive state.
