## Brief

Found by the planner-side cross-vendor review of PR #1160 at fd8bfdc947bb (store-sqlite reviewer): P2, confirmed. The owner deferred it to after the #1160 merge (2026-10-09); fix it on integration/beta-next.

**Finding.** Every native invocation runs several full scans over lifetime-append-only history (authority_global_commits, native dispatch ledger) under the write lock

**Where.** `crates/platform/chio-store-sqlite/src/admission_operation_store/security_participant_state/egress/write.rs:345` (introduced by 7e54c14a6)

**Scenario.** footprint() in egress/write.rs, output/write.rs and nonce_preflight/write.rs executes '(SELECT COUNT(*) FROM authority_global_commits)' plus COUNT(*) over each journal table, and each writer calls it three times. authority_global_commits receives a row for every admission/budget/migration commit store-wide and has a no-delete trigger. dispatch_ledger::storage::verify_coverage additionally scans every native dispatch ledger row and every native_dispatch_ledger global commit on each retain, capture, ledger load and native return check. A native call therefore does on the order of ten O(lifetime) scans while holding the store mutex and an IMMEDIATE transaction, making aggregate work quadratic in store age; this contradicts integrity.rs:122-123 ('Ordinary readback must not turn retained history into lifetime work on every invocation').

**Evidence at fd8bfdc.** egress/write.rs:348-350 '(SELECT COUNT(*) FROM authority_global_commits)' in footprint, used at lines 202, 240, 295, 310; output/write.rs:262-266 and nonce_preflight/write.rs:242-246 same pattern; dispatch_ledger/storage.rs:81-94 'EXISTS(SELECT 1 FROM admission_operation_native_dispatch_ledger AS record WHERE ... (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM authority_global_commits ...) != 1) OR EXISTS(SELECT 1 FROM authority_global_commits ...)'.
