**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bound lost-transaction retries before requeueing**

When a persistent `SQLITE_FULL`, `SQLITE_IOERR`, or similar fault causes SQLite to roll back the whole transaction, this branch requeues every member as `Retry` without incrementing the `member_fault_retries` counter. The retry-exhaustion transition to `StoreUnavailable` exists only in the transaction-still-active branch below, so an auto-rollback fault can spin and retain every caller indefinitely without raising the store incident or invoking M19's fail-closed response. Count whole-transaction losses toward a bounded retry policy as well, with the stop-intent latch continuing to protect priority stop requests.
