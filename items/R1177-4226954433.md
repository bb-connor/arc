**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Require sender constraints for the M1 tool call**

Require DPoP or an equivalent sender-bound profile for this cross-host call and qualify missing, substituted, and replayed proofs.
