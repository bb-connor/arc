**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Track resync state per stream before clearing it**

When a session has multiple broadcast consumers, such as an attached GET stream and an active POST stream, A7 records only one session-wide coalesced resync flag and skipped count, without the requesting stream's identity. Consequently, after one consumer reaches this threshold, the server cannot clear “that consumer's” flag: clearing the global flag can discard another consumer's independently pending resync and silently lose its notifications, while retaining it keeps emitting passes after the lagging stream is gone. Make the resync cursor, pass counter, and pending flag consumer-scoped, or preserve the shared request until every requesting consumer is resolved.
