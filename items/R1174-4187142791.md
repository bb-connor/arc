**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define a valid record for journal-bypass offline stops**

Fresh evidence after adding the offline direct-append fallback is that it skips the intent journal and then appends a `Stop`, while S2 requires every `Stop` or `Restrict` record to carry `satisfies_intent` for an actual journal entry. When the journal is full or its write fails, no `StopIntentRef` exists, so the writer must either fabricate one or produce a record that the specified verifier rejects; the offline stop therefore cannot become durable in precisely the crash-loop scenario this fallback is intended to cover. Define a separately verifiable no-intent offline record form, or persist a valid intent before appending.
