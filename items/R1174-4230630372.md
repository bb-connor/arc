**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind quorum records to the roster generation they used**

When the operator roster or threshold rotates after a quorum-authorized `Resume` or `Relax`, a later boot or offline verifier cannot revalidate this historical record: `Quorum` stores only an artifact digest and principals, while S29 tells verifiers to use the active signed deployment configuration and reject an older roster. Using the new roster can invalidate a legitimate chain, while accepting an old roster provides no signed proof that it was active for this transition. Carry the quorum's `roster_digest` and deployment generation in the signed authorizer or record and retain the corresponding authenticated configuration.
