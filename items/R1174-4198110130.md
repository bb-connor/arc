**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Keep end markers until the client acknowledges the event**

When the transport reports that a `SubscriptionEnded` frame was flushed but the TCP connection breaks before the client observes it, this rule permits deleting the durable marker. A crash or reconnect then has nothing to requeue, while the session remains active and the client cannot distinguish the ended subscription from a quiet one, contradicting the at-least-once guarantee below. Treat a flush as handoff only and retain the marker until a later `Last-Event-ID` proves the client passed `end_event_id`, or add an explicit acknowledgement.
