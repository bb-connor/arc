**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Preserve receipt creation time after tool execution**

For a long-running tool call, taking authority time before the effect boundary backdates the signed receipt by the full execution duration. This conflicts with the repository's definition of the receipt timestamp as the time the receipt was created (`spec/HTTP-SUBSTRATE.md:352`) and can corrupt audit ordering and timestamp-based consumers. Keep this fallible read in the typed post-effect path, or record separate start and receipt-creation timestamps.
