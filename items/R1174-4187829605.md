**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make unreadable-journal bypasses retire pending intents**

Fresh evidence after the pending-intent fix is the unreadable-journal fallback here: if the journal later becomes readable, its old entry is still unsatisfied because the bypass record has an empty `subsumes_intents`. Boot then cannot apply that entry, since the bypass head is already at least as narrow and S2 makes a same-state stop a no-op while `Restrict` must narrow; the entry therefore blocks readiness and every later `Resume` or `Relax` indefinitely. Either persist a recoverable proof that the bypass subsumes the unread entries or do not report a durable bypass until the journal can be reconciled.
