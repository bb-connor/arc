**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Persist terminalization before hand-off**

The durable `Ended` marker is created only when drain hands the notification to the transport, leaving an earlier crash window after in-memory terminalization but before hand-off. For example, if a subscription ends because authority time is unavailable or its subject disappears and the process crashes before the next drain, the resume record still contains `Live`; if the clock recovers or the subject is recreated before restore, A25 can reauthorize and resurrect the subscription without ever delivering the already-owed `SubscriptionEnded`. Persist the `Ended` state when terminalization occurs, before waiting for transport capacity, and then retain it through emission.
