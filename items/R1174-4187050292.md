**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Trigger a pending hint for cross-process revocations**

When a capability is revoked in another process while its subscription is otherwise quiet, polling the revocation head only refreshes the authority state; it does not create a pending event. H7 revalidates only at drain time before an existing hint is delivered, so no drain is triggered and the subscriber may never receive either `Changed` or `SubscriptionEnded`, despite H10's durable-cursor guarantee. The revocation poll must post a lineage-matched capability hint (as H10's tail rule describes), or a periodic scan must explicitly terminalize affected subscriptions.
