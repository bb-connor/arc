**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Exempt quarantined successors from parent-state inheritance**

When this remedy is invoked for a tainted parent, I7a requires child creation to inherit the parent's complete observation set and explicitly guarantees that the child is never less tainted than the parent. The isolated successor therefore retains the parent's `External` or `unknown` influence and cannot satisfy the `BoundedExternal` requirement in step 4, even after receiving only typed P5 returns. Define the verified isolation-epoch transition as an explicit, fail-closed exception to parent inheritance, with only the P5 return crossing into the new epoch, or this advertised automated remedy always denies.
