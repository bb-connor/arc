**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make check ordinals restart-stable**

When a serving owner restarts without an intervening authority commit, the new process can observe the same head `c` as its predecessor, but the spec provides no durable or restart-stable source for `check_ordinal`. A process-local ordinal can therefore reuse `(store_uuid, c, k)`, leaving two check-only receipts with identical comparison keys and violating X4's claimed total order. Persist the per-head ordinal or add a restart-stable unique tie-breaker.
