**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Reserve durable capacity for a final stop**

Because the chain starts with epoch 1 as `Stopped` and alternates states, reaching the 65,536-record limit leaves the durable head `Running`. The next stop only latches tier 1 in memory, while an operation already past its early check reaches S7, reads the durable `Running` head, and may cross; a restart also has no committed stop to reload. Reserve durable capacity for a terminal stop or persist an exhausted-and-stopped marker so exhaustion cannot turn the authoritative stop into a best-effort latch.
