**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bound and paginate issuance request listings**

After approved and denied rows accumulate, this query returns every matching record, including the stored presentation and challenge JSON, into one vector and response. The pending-count limit does not cap terminal history, so a long-lived service can eventually allocate and serialize an unbounded amount of attacker-influenced data when an operator lists history. Add owner-bound pagination and retention/size limits rather than exposing an unrestricted `ORDER BY` query.
