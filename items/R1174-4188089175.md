**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Journal the full terminal transition payload**

Fresh evidence after the journal-ordering fix is that `pending_terminal` persists only `(session_id, terminal_generation)`. If the host crashes after this fsync but before the SQLite transition commits, boot cannot reconstruct the required `event_id`, termination `reason`, or `retention_deadline`, so it cannot complete the promised tombstone exactly; it must either leave the store unavailable indefinitely or fence the session while losing/corrupting the retained terminal replay. Persist the complete terminal-transition payload in the journal before proceeding.
