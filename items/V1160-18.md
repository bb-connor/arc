## Brief

Found by the planner-side cross-vendor review of PR #1160 at fd8bfdc947bb (store-sqlite reviewer): P2, confirmed. The owner deferred it to after the #1160 merge (2026-10-09); fix it on integration/beta-next.

**Finding.** Every load of a terminal admission operation re-verifies the entire admission commit chain (O(total history) per read, under the store mutex and often the write lock)

**Where.** `crates/platform/chio-store-sqlite/src/admission_operation_store/projection/terminal_time.rs:39` (introduced by f23efdf43)

**Scenario.** load_by_replay_key_tx and load_by_operation_id_tx build a fresh CheckedHistoryScope per call. For any terminal operation they reach load_verified_terminal_receipt_with_history -> terminal_time::verify -> history.qualify, which runs verify_admission_commit_chronology (COUNT/MAX/NOT EXISTS over all commits) and verify_admission_commit_chain (reads every admission_operation_commits row and recomputes a canonical-JSON SHA-256 per row). The commit table is append-only and never pruned. Concrete path: a client retries an already-completed tool call; begin()/begin_with_retained_tool_request opens an IMMEDIATE transaction and calls begin_prepared_operation_tx -> load_by_replay_key_tx, so the full-history hash walk runs while holding SQLite's write lock and the store connection mutex. The same O(all history) walk runs for every load_by_operation_id_tx of a terminal operation (for example ReceiptStore::load_chio_receipt, load_retained_tool_request, the history loaders, load_terminal_replay) and once per call/page in the batch paths that share a scope. With a store holding millions of commits each such call costs seconds and serializes every other admission behind it; repeated retries of completed request ids or receipt lookups become a cost amplifier against the whole kernel. Before f23efdf43 this check was O(1) (committed_at vs updated_at).
