Fresh evidence after the terminalization fix is the newly specified persistence-failure path: if this re-sign fails and the process crashes before a retry, the existing durable resume record still identifies the subscription as `Live`. If the transient clock failure clears or the missing subject is recreated before restart, A25 can reauthorize that stale record and resurrect the subscription without delivering the owed `SubscriptionEnded`, contrary to the guarantee at line 517. A failed `Ended` persist must keep the session unavailable or durably invalidate the old resume record rather than relying on an in-memory retry.
