**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Prevent restore after terminal persistence fails**

Fresh evidence after adding terminal-tombstone persistence is that this failure branch continues termination without durably invalidating the previous active resume record. For a non-expiry termination such as an explicit close or fail-closed teardown, a crash after this failed write can therefore restore that still-valid record and serve the terminated session again; A19 only keeps a failed restore inactive in the current boot and does not create a durable fence. Treat this like H5a's double-failure path by making the session store not ready or retaining a mandatory terminal-transition retry until the old record is durably fenced.
