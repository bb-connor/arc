**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Include PID and session in incident identity**

The normative repair contract defines an incident as including both session identity and PID/start identity, but this proposed value omits the session and PID while `incident_matches` accepts simple equality over the remaining fields. Two same-build processes started in the same clock tick, or records from different sessions, can therefore compare equal and cause diagnostics to be attributed to the wrong crash. Add the selected PID and session identity to this structure and to the mismatch tests.
