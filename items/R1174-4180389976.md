**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make synthesized request IDs unique across payloads**

For providers without call IDs, `{provider}_{name}_call_{index}` is unique only within one payload because the index restarts on every lift. The shipped bridge unconditionally copies `provenance.request_id` into `ToolCallRequest.request_id` (`crates/kernel/chio-kernel/src/provider_verdict.rs:38-55`), while durable admission retains `(request_namespace_digest, request_id)` for the namespace lifetime. Consequently, a later Ollama or Gemini call to the same tool at the same index either replays the earlier terminal result when its binding matches or conflicts when its arguments differ. Include a per-payload or conversation-unique component rather than merely the batch index.
