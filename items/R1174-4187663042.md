**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define a timestamp for clockless incident openings**

When break-glass is needed because authority time was unavailable at the opening `Stop` or `Migration`, that record has `decided_at = Unavailable` and `StopEpochV1` contains no other Unix commit timestamp. The required comparison against "the opening record's commit" therefore has no authoritative value to evaluate: a fail-closed implementation rejects every break-glass attempt in this core scenario, while consulting a local database timestamp would violate the signed authority-time model. Persist an authoritative opening-time bound or define the check against another signed observation.
