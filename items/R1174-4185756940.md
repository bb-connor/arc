**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Reject equal-position origin forks before renewing the lease**

When S34's whole-volume restore produces a different signed record at the same `(chain_generation, epoch)`, this acceptance logic checks only that the position did not regress and ignores `origin_head_digest`. A shard whose local record at that position is Running can therefore renew its lease against an origin whose forked equal-position record is Stopped, never enter the Behind path, and continue admitting work indefinitely. This is distinct from the replayed-heartbeat case because the nonce and signature are both fresh; require matching digests whenever the positions are equal and latch on any mismatch.
