**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind break-glass time attestations to the incident**

When authority time is unavailable, these rules allow a quorum artifact merely because it also carries a signed time attestation, without requiring that attestation to name the authority, scope, expected stop epoch, or a freshness value relative to the opening stop or persisted time floor. Because the quorum approvals in S29 also do not cover `time_attestation`, an old valid attestation can be grafted onto a newly signed quorum artifact and bypass the fail-closed clock gate. Define the attestation payload and verify its incident binding and freshness inside the resume transaction.
