**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Persist terminal hints before waiting for stream flushes**

If the host crashes after `terminate` writes the terminal into the in-memory `SessionEventLog` but before an attached stream flushes it, this promised replay cannot be reconstructed: A24 persists subscription end markers, but no persisted record defined here carries the terminal event id, reason, retention deadline, or replay-consumed state, and the terminal tombstone prevents the session from being restored as active. The reconnect therefore receives only the terminal-state response and silently misses the `Terminal` event; persist the terminal hint before beginning the flush wait and serve the retained replay from that durable tombstone.
