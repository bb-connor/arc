**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define stop behavior when artifact signing fails**

`SigningBackend::sign_bytes` is fallible, but the failure table has no signing case and this requirement does not say whether signing occurs before or after the stop-chain commit. Signing first can make an emergency stop fail to become durable when the signer is unavailable; committing first can leave a transition permanently without the signed evidence promised here. Specify a fail-closed protocol, such as committing the stop while retaining a durable signing obligation and blocking resume/readiness until reconciliation, while refusing a resume whose artifact cannot be signed.
