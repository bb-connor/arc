**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Refuse provider routes without an enforceable output ceiling**

When the fixed Codex subscription route is selected, the pinned research explicitly says it has no enforceable hard output-token ceiling and that disconnecting does not prove provider computation or billing stopped (`research/chio-readiness.md:116`), but this task only asks implementations to display unavailable provider-limit dimensions. That permits the required protected Codex profile to qualify while a task can continue consuming unbounded provider tokens or spend outside Chio's resource admission. Require an enforceable route-level output ceiling (and conservative reservation for costs that cannot be known exactly) or keep that provider route unavailable rather than merely reporting the missing limit.
