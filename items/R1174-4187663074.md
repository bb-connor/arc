**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Cancel timed-out resume and relax writes**

When a `Resume` or `Relax` encounters repeated `SQLITE_FULL` or `IOERR`, this rule returns `stop_not_durable` after two seconds but keeps the widening write queued. Unlike `Stop` and `Restrict`, those transitions have no intent latch, so if the store recovers and the expected head has not moved, the queued operation can later commit and re-enable execution after the operator was told it failed. Restrict queued-after-timeout behavior to narrowing transitions, and cancel or return an outcome-unknown status for timed-out widening transitions.
