**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind approval events to their owning session**

Fresh evidence after adding the approval-store cursor is that events with no `operation_id` are routed from `subject_id` to “the session that created” the request, but the current `ApprovalRequest` and `chio_hitl_pending` record contain no session or request-namespace identity. If the same agent subject has two sessions, a tailer recovering after the source crashes cannot determine which session owns the approval and must either miss the durable hint or expose it to the wrong session; persist an audience owner reference in the event transaction and route by that value.
