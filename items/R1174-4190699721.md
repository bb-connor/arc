**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Advance the epoch when terminalizing after re-sign**

When a standard subscription fails re-authorization, A25 runs after A17 has already persisted generation `g`, so a terminal transition using epoch `g` does not exceed the active generation as the store requires. A permanently expired or revoked subscription will therefore leave the record inactive and repeat the same failed sequence on every restart, instead of producing the promised terminal-state response. Either re-authorize before persisting `g` or use a terminal epoch greater than `g`.
