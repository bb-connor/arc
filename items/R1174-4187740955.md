**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Exclude sealed D1 permits from delegation-root fences**

When a D1-sealed call is admitted in the same serving authority that later closes its delegation root, this row records the root on the operation, and rule 3's exact join then refuses its dispatch or release once the closure fence exists. That directly violates section 6.2's invariant that a `DelegationRoot` closure stops only unsealed work and that already sealed permits remain executable, potentially stranding work the receiver is required to honor. Do not attach the closure-fenced root reference to operations backed by an already sealed permit, or distinguish sealed and unsealed references in the fence predicate.
