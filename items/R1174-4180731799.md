**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Allow replacement of an unused failed successor**

The unique index consumes the predecessor's sole successor slot when the workflow is created, before the continuation captures or executes. If the newly issued capability expires during approval, is revoked before capture, or otherwise makes that workflow permanently unable to capture, creating a corrected successor is still rejected even though no continuation used the authority, leaving the original fault irrecoverable. Enforce uniqueness at successful capture or permit replacement after a successor reaches a terminal state without consuming its continuation.
