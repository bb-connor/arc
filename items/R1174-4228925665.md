**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Add retention for approval hint events**

On a long-lived approval store, every pending, resolution, reservation, cancellation, and threshold transition appends a `chio_hitl_events` row, while the mandatory triggers reject every update and delete and no checkpoint, retention horizon, or compaction mechanism is defined. This operational hint log therefore grows permanently with approval traffic and can eventually exhaust the SQLite store even after all approvals and sessions that needed the events are gone. Define safe pruning below durable consumer watermarks or compact old events into a committed checkpoint rather than making the table undeletable forever.
