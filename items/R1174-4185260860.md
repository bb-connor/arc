**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Carry the chain generation through rollover**

This rollover cannot be represented by `StopEpochV1`: the signed body and head key contain only `(authority_id, scope, epoch)`, while epochs start at 1 and shard freshness compares only epochs. If a new generation resets the epoch, it creates indistinguishable signed epoch identities and a shard holding the old high epoch will consider itself ahead forever; if it does not reset, the 65,536-record bound remains exhausted and rollover creates no headroom. Add a signed generation identifier to records, keys, expected-head checks, artifacts, and shard replication comparisons.
