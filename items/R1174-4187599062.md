**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Remove satisfied intents before allowing resume**

If removal of an already satisfied journal entry fails while the process remains alive, this rule permits `Resume` or `Relax` because it blocks only on an *unsatisfied* entry. The widening transition then makes the old entry unsatisfied under the predicate above (`Resume`/`Relax` follows its satisfying record, or the head is running), so the next boot honors and reapplies that stale intent, unexpectedly stopping or re-restricting a scope that was validly resumed. Remove and fsync satisfied entries under the journal mutex before committing a widening transition, or refuse widening while any entry remains.
