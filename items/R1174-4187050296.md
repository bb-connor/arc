**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bound resync bursts below the broadcast capacity**

A session may have the default 256 resource subscriptions, while a resync burst also emits up to three catalog notifications and a logging warning. That burst can therefore exceed the 256-event broadcast capacity; for a lagging consumer, the synchronous burst itself causes another `Lagged`, which schedules the same oversized burst again, so the claimed coalescing does not guarantee convergence. Chunk or summarize resyncs, reserve capacity for the non-resource events, or lower the subscription bound so one complete burst always fits.
