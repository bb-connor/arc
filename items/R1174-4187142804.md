**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind integrity audit references to the replay namespace**

The replay identity is `(request_namespace_digest, request_id)`, and the same request id may legally be reused in another authenticated namespace, but this supposedly per-receipt reference binds only the raw request id; I25a likewise records the audit basis under `request_id` alone. If one context can issue requests under two namespaces while its influence commitment is unchanged, the two receipts get the same `commitment_ref`, defeating the stated unlinkability and leaving the raw audit rows without the identity needed to distinguish them. Include the authenticated namespace (and preferably the receipt id) in both the HMAC input and the audit record identity.
