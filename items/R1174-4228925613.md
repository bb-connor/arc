**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Prove the receipt signer is still active**

When the signer frozen at admission has since been retired or compromised, membership in `trusted_kernel_keys` or an authority's key history does not prove that `kernel_key` is the current boot-selected signer. The holder of that old key can sign a forged finalization, copy the known frozen identity into `original_signer`, satisfy every listed check, and even appear not to be a rotated signer, bypassing the interim withhold rule for release or waiver receipts. Bind verification to an authenticated signer generation or validity/revocation state and reject keys that were not active for this finalization.
