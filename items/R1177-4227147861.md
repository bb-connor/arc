On every successful Secret Service lookup, `output.stdout.to_vec()` copies the seed into an ordinary `String`, and `text.trim().to_string()` makes another copy before only the latter is wrapped in `Zeroizing`. The first `String` is dropped without wiping, leaving the organization signing seed in allocator memory despite the zeroizing API; keep the UTF-8 buffer itself in a zeroizing wrapper before trimming or avoid the non-zeroizing intermediate.
