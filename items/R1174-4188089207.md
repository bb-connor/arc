**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Leave pending-approval submissions unbound**

Fresh evidence after the verdict-binding fix is the `PendingApproval` path: spec 9 explicitly omits `IdentityDisposition` for `PendingApproval`, while the provider fabric has only `Allow` and `Deny` and lowers the pending response as a deny. This fallback therefore treats that pending receipt as `Terminal` and binds the submission to it; when approval later resolves, the real terminal or allow receipt has a different id and fails `ReceiptAlreadyBound`, so approval-gated provider calls can never complete. Exclude signed `PendingApproval` decisions from this fallback or represent a pending disposition explicitly.
