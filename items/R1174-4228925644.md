**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bound quorum approvals before encoding stop records**

When a scope has a large operator roster, a valid quorum artifact may contain arbitrarily many distinct approvals because both `approvals` and the recorded `Quorum.principals` are unbounded vectors and verification accepts at least `k`, rather than exactly a bounded set. Such an artifact can exceed the spec's 4 KiB `StopEpochV1` limit, leaving an otherwise authorized resume or relax unable to produce a valid chain record and keeping the scope stopped until the caller guesses a smaller subset. Define a maximum roster/quorum size and reject oversized artifacts before transition construction, or canonicalize the record to exactly `k` verified principals.
