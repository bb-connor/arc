**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Add a durable cursor for budget changes**

When a `BudgetStore` mutation crosses a subscribed threshold and its source component dies after commit but before the in-process post, H10 promises that the delivering process will recover the hint by tailing the source cursor. However, the cursor table has no `Budget` entry even though budget subscriptions and `ThresholdCrossed` are supported, so a still-running delivery host has no durable head or sequence to tail and can silently miss the change until an unrelated restart or re-subscription. Specify the budget store's durable mutation cursor and its tail mapping, or exclude budget hints from this crash-safety claim.
