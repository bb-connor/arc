**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Append a signed receipt for an operator denial**

When B's operator denies an issuance request, this path only updates the mutable inbox row after consuming the challenge; it never invokes a receipt signer or appends the decision to the receipt log. The denial therefore cannot be independently verified or included as tamper-evident evidence, contrary to Chio's decision-recording boundary; persist the signed denial receipt before returning success and bind it to the challenge, subject, operator attribution, and reason.
