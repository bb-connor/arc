**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Tail approvals from their actual durable store**

For deployments where approvals are committed through the separate `ApprovalStore`, resolving an approval does not necessarily append an admission projection to `authority_global_commits`; section 12.3 likewise says approval hints originate directly from the approval store. Tailing only the admission store's sequence can therefore miss an approval permanently when the source crashes after its commit but before the in-process post, unless the delivering process also happens to restart and emit the generic catch-up. Give the approval store its own durable monotonic cursor or transactionally project every approval commit into the listed admission chain.
