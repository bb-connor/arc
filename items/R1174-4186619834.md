**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Verify the witnessed stop-chain digest**

The optional whole-volume rollback check compares only `(chain_generation, epoch)`. A restored database can fork from an older snapshot and later append a different sequence until its numeric position equals or exceeds the witnessed position, at which point this check accepts even though the witness's signed stop record is absent from the local chain; that also permits two artifacts with the same record identity, the risk this check is meant to detect. Persist the witnessed record digest and require that exact `(id, digest)` to occur in the local chain ancestry before readiness.
