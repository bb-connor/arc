**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Treat worker profile as an authorization fact**

This declares `worker_profile` to be evidence that no guard or policy may use, but integrity rule I7 derives the calling context's initial influence from this exact field: a `split_domain` value starts trusted and can therefore satisfy `RequiredIntegrity` at the authoritative dispatch crossing. Implementing this rule makes integrity gating unusable, while implementing I7 silently turns the profile into an allow-affecting fact. Reclassify it as a verified fact source with explicit qualification and failure behavior, or introduce a separate attested input for integrity admission.
