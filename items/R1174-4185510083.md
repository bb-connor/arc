**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make automatic rollover records representable**

An automatic `ChainRollover` has no request, yet `StopEpochV1` requires `authorizer` and `reason_commitment` to equal `contributors[0]`, and its documented `requested_via` values contain no automatic-writer source. Consequently the writer cannot construct the rollover mandated here without inventing a fake request, reusing the triggering request inaccurately, or violating the signed record invariant. Define a system rollover source and an explicit contributor/reason rule for this transition, or make those fields transition-specific.
