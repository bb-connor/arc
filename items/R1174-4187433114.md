**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Check the opening-stop observation for cooldown resumes**

When an incident has acquired a `Restrict` or `Rollover` record, the current head differs from the opening `Stop` whose observation S19 says starts `SamePrincipalAfter`. Requiring the observation for the head's exact epoch either rejects the valid opening-stop observation or restarts the cooldown from a later record, so repeated restrictions or rollovers can indefinitely delay an otherwise authorized resume; bind and verify the opening stop id instead.
