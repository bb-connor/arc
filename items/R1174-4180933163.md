**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Index process-tree ancestors for closure fences**

Each operation records only its own `process_lineage`, while closing a `ProcessTree(root)` installs a fence for the root lineage and expects an exact refs-to-fences join in the dispatch CAS. For an operation belonging to a descendant lineage, that join does not match; if its liveness guard reads before the separate process cancellation commits and its dispatch CAS runs afterward, it can still become `DispatchCommitted` after the tree closure. Record every ancestor lineage as an authority ref, or make the in-transaction fence predicate perform a durable ancestor lookup.
