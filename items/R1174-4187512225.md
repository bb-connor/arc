**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Preserve satisfied intents across rollover**

If applying a pending intent leaves the generation in the rollover margin, S6 makes a `Rollover` the next commit, and a failed or interrupted journal removal can leave that already-applied entry present while the current head is the rollover. Because rollover records must carry `satisfies_intent: None`, this head-only predicate then misclassifies the fulfilled entry as unsatisfied; boot cannot append the promised narrowing `Restrict` when containment is already equally narrow, and `Resume` remains blocked by `StopIntentPending`. Treat a matching record in the head's ancestry as satisfaction when every later record preserves an equally narrow state, or carry the satisfaction proof through rollover.
