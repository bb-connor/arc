**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Preserve ended subscriptions during restore**

After adding the `Ended` resume-record arm, this restore rule still treats every persisted entry as a fresh subscription and drops entries that fail reauthorization. When a capability expired or was revoked before a crash, restore can therefore discard the durable end marker (or attempt to resurrect it as live), and A26 emits an ordinary resource update instead of the owed `SubscriptionEnded`. Branch on the tagged state: requeue existing `Ended` markers, and terminalize failed `Live` entries without sending catch-up updates for them.
