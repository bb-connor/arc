**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind the successor scope into ordinal reservation**

The specified `reserve_successor_ordinal(predecessor_workflow, resolution_attempt_id)` call and its `Reserved` link carry no successor scope, yet this rule requires the reservation transaction to check that scope's stop head and authorize `Create` for its deployment. A caller can therefore reserve while the eventual successor scope is stopped because the writer has no authenticated value to check, mutating and occupying the claim despite the `deny` disposition. Pass and persist the successor scope during reservation, then require `CreateWorkflow` to use that same scope.
