**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Continuously fence shards that lose the stop origin**

A tenant shard that boots while caught up can remain live after losing connectivity to the pool shard. If a kernel stop then commits at the origin, acknowledgement deliberately does not wait for that shard, while the only origin comparison specified here occurs at boot; the live shard therefore continues authorizing crossings against its stale local replica and has no way to discover that it should become not-ready. Require a renewable origin-freshness lease or bounded continuous polling that makes an already-serving shard fail closed when it misses fan-out.
