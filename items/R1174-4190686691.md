<a href="#"><img alt="P1" src="https://greptile-static-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/badges/p1.svg?v=9" align="top"></a> **Restore cannot terminalize session**

When a standard subscription fails re-authorization during restore, A25 attempts to terminalize the session at generation `g`. A17 has already persisted that same generation, but the terminal store requires a strictly newer one. The transition therefore fails, so reconnect will not receive the promised durable `subscription_not_restored` terminal-state response. Use a newer terminal epoch or check subscriptions before persisting `g`.
