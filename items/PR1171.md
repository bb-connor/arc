1. **P1: Plain delegation bypasses the normative scope-hash requirement.**  
   **Location:** [draft, lines 833-841](https://github.com/bb-connor/arc/blob/f3a921aa6913/spec/ietf/draft-whelan-chio-protocol.md#L833-L841).  
   **Claim:** A plain delegation chain does not trigger chain binding.  
   **Evidence:** `origin/main:spec/PROTOCOL.md:507-516` requires signed `scope_hash` on every hop and rejects chains omitting it. The draft instead permits a correctly signed plain chain without those hashes. `crates/core/chio-core-types/src/capability/token.rs:451-458` and `crates/kernel/chio-kernel-core/src/capability_verify.rs:600-618` confirm this compatibility behavior. It matches the implementation but contradicts the designated normative spec, producing different acceptance decisions between vendors. Reconcile the compatibility exception with the normative contract.

2. **P1: Receipt algorithm-hint validation becomes conditional.**  
   **Location:** [draft, lines 1144-1147](https://github.com/bb-connor/arc/blob/f3a921aa6913/spec/ietf/draft-whelan-chio-protocol.md#L1144-L1147).  
   **Claim:** Check the envelope hint for consistency when a cryptographic floor is applied.  
   **Evidence:** `origin/main:spec/PROTOCOL.md:983-985` requires every present hint to match the signature prefix. Because the hint is unsigned, adding `algorithm: "p256"` to an otherwise valid Ed25519 receipt leaves its signature intact. The ordinary helper at `crates/core/chio-core-types/src/receipt/body.rs:479-494` accepts it; only the floor-aware helper at lines 521-528 checks the hint. The draft must distinguish this implementation limitation from the unconditional normative requirement.

3. **P2: Privacy analysis omits disclosure through predictable output hashes.**  
   **Location:** [draft, lines 4036-4052](https://github.com/bb-connor/arc/blob/f3a921aa6913/spec/ietf/draft-whelan-chio-protocol.md#L4036-L4052).  
   **Missing consideration:** Omitting raw output does not conceal low-entropy results.  
   **Evidence:** Draft lines 1007-1012 and `crates/kernel/chio-kernel/src/kernel/responses/receipt_persistence.rs:40` define deterministic, unsalted output hashes. A receipt reader can recover a boolean result by comparing two candidate hashes, even when arguments contain no sensitive data. The signing nonce does not salt `content_hash`. Document this disclosure risk and its implications for receipt sharing; [RFC 3552 §5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3552.html#section-5) calls for explaining residual risks after mitigations.
