**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Account for in-flight settlements before closing**

When a delivery refusal meets an `InFlight` payment, the journal may remain `ReconcileFailed` indefinitely if the external rail keeps failing, but this rule still signs the `Closed` artifact immediately on the assumption that the intent completes on its own. Because `stranded_final.retained_delivery_obligations` includes only `Open` holds, that immutable artifact then omits the outstanding in-flight hold and settlement. Either wait for this payment stage to become `Final`, or include in-flight delivery obligations in the final stranded-capacity evidence.
