**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Fence operation creation before closing the drain**

When a new durable operation begins after the drain has enumerated the authority-ref index, `begin` still inserts its operation and refs because the closure predicate is checked only at dispatch and release. Such an operation can park in `ApprovalRequired` after the one drain has passed it, and the closure can sign `Closed` without that nonterminal operation in its drain ledger; the later dispatch fence prevents execution but does not withdraw its approval or make the signed closure inventory complete. Reject `begin` against an existing matching fence, or make the final enumeration/CAS exclude concurrent inserts.
