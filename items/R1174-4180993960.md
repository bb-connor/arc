**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make stopped non-durable effects terminal to retries**

When a `NonDurable` side-effecting call executes and a stop lands before release, this row drops the output and leaves no operation row or custody record. The stop rules nevertheless describe `KernelStopped` as temporary and retryable after resume, but spec 5 explicitly confirms that retrying a non-durable call redispatches it; a caller following that guidance can therefore perform the side effect twice. Force these calls onto a durable path, or return a terminal non-retryable/outcome-unknown result rather than the generic temporary `Withheld` response used for replayable durable calls and safe reads.
