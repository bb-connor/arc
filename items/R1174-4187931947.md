**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Align bypass field rules with intent retirement**

Fresh evidence after the bypass-reconciliation changes is that this normative field rule still says an offline bypass retires no journal entry, while S25 now relies on that record's `subsumes_intents` or `subsumes_unread` proof to satisfy and remove pending entries. An implementation following S2 can preserve those entries indefinitely, leaving later `Resume` or `Relax` blocked by `StopIntentPending` or causing a stale intent to be reapplied. Update the field rule to define the new proof-driven retirement and its validation.
