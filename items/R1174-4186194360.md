**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Persist the end hint until transport delivery is durable**

Fresh evidence after the terminalized-subscription fix is this remaining crash window: the subscription and its persisted metadata are removed as soon as `drain` hands `SubscriptionEnded` to the in-memory transport queue. If the delivering process crashes before that queue emits the frame, restore has neither the queued hint nor the subscription from which H5a says it will re-derive the end, so the promised at-least-once end notification can disappear. Retain a durable terminal marker until emission is acknowledged, or persist a delivery cursor that lets restore replay the end event.
