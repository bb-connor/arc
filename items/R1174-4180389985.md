**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make subscription arming atomic with source changes**

`SessionEventLog::subscribe` receives a caller-sampled `SourceLevel` while the authoritative source is external to the pure data structure. If the source commits and `post` runs after that sample but before the subscription is inserted, `post` sees no subscriber and the subsequent subscribe sees the stale clean level, silently losing the change despite H4 and H8. Subscribe under the source's synchronization boundary, or insert first and compare a monotonic source revision afterward so this interleaving either queues a hint or forces resynchronization.
