**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Retain subcase suffixes in range expansion**

When a document abbreviates defined subcases as `H06a-H06b`, `CASE_ID` finds both valid endpoints, but this expansion discards their suffixes and additionally inserts `H06`. Since the case table defines `H06a` and `H06b` rather than a base `H06`, otherwise valid documentation fails the gate with an undefined-case error; retain suffixes when parsing ranges or avoid numeric expansion for same-number suffixed ranges.
