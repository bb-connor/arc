**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Persist subscription IDs in the resume record**

The persisted `Live` and `Ended` variants omit `subscription_id`, even though restored end markers use `(subscription_id, reason)` as their logical identity and Part B returns that ID from `chio/events/subscribe` and requires it for `unsubscribe`. After a restart, the server therefore cannot reconstruct the client-visible ID: generating a new one leaves the client's stored ID unusable, while deriving one from `uri`/`subject` is unspecified and cannot distinguish duplicate subscriptions. Include the subscription ID in both signed variants before claiming that subscriptions survive restore.
