**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Compensate no-row NonDurable calls before returning**

When `StoreUnavailable` is returned for a `NonDurable` call's pre-dispatch `CheckOnlyCrossing`, this row signs a reusable denial and ends the attempt while explicitly performing no compensation. Unlike a check-only read, a `NonDurable` call can already own the in-memory ledger entries described in section 4.4, including invocation, budget, payment, credential, or runtime reservations; M10 explicitly releases these on an equivalent pre-dispatch `Overloaded` result. Release those driver-local entries before returning the denial, or they remain stranded even though the crossing is known not to have committed.
