**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Keep the resync cursor per consumer**

Fresh evidence that the prior per-stream fix is incomplete is this remaining normative sentence, which still makes `resync_cursor` per-session even though A7 assigns each consumer its own pass cursor and A8 requires their records to advance and clear independently. When two attached streams request resync at different points, a later consumer can inherit the earlier consumer's advanced cursor and complete only the suffix of the pass, missing the catalog or URI re-read hints before that position. Store and advance the cursor in each `consumer_id`'s resync record instead.
