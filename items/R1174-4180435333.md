**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define an idempotent integrity join**

This is not a lattice join: `join(s, s)` doubles `bounded_bits_total`, and an ordered hash such as `H(s1.commitment, s2.commitment)` is generally neither commutative nor associative. Consequently, the mandated lattice-law tests cannot pass, and replaying or rebuilding the same delivery can spuriously push a context over its bit bound. Track uniquely identified observations or derive the commitment and total from a canonical deduplicated set.
