**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind invalidation events to their native operation**

When one task has proposals from multiple native operations or owners, `task_id` plus the owner-local `proposal_id` cannot identify which review was invalidated. Both `review.open` and `approval.submit` require an owner-bound `operation_ref`, but this event omits it, so a client can invalidate the wrong matching review or leave the stale one actionable. Include the native operation reference in `approval_invalidated` and its fixture.
