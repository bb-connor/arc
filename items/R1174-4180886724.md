**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Treat a committed capture as post-dispatch at a cut**

When an authority cut observes `CapturePending` with `capture = Committed`, this row instructs the machine to finish the intent commit even though the newly installed closure fence must refuse that dispatch. The refusal then falls into M10's generic slow-path policy row, which compensates under `PreDispatchNoEffect`, contradicting T3 and spec 4's explicit rule that a committed capture is post-dispatch and must never be compensated. This can either incorrectly release captured funds or leave the drain without a legal transition; classify this state as post-dispatch with its hold frozen instead.
