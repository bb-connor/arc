When an update or rollback changes the accepted native component generation while clients remain connected, this lifecycle case checks for a coherent installed tuple but does not exercise requests, queued responses, or subscriptions over already-authenticated old channels. An old client or surviving helper can therefore continue receiving protected bytes or dispatching actions after the new generation is fenced, even though fresh connections correctly reject it; keep channels open through each update transition and require the native owner to close or revalidate both peers, current authority, and generation before any later protected release or dispatch.
