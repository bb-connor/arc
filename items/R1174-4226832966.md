**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define signer-rotation behavior in the landed specs**

Implementations can therefore bind the original identity yet disagree on whether a current-key finalization is authorized to release output. Specify the current-key/original-identity verification and the resulting delivery disposition in a landed normative document.
