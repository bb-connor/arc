**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind close requests to the receiving authority**

When the same principal or capability is recognized by two serving authorities, a valid non-operator close request accepted by authority A can be replayed within its 60-second lifetime to authority B because the signed body contains neither `coordinator_authority_id` nor `request_namespace_digest`, and nonce reservation is local to each receiver. This can permanently fence and drain the corresponding capability subtree or other colliding local space at a receiver the signer never selected. Include the target authority and authenticated namespace (or an equivalent store identity) in the signed request and verify them before reserving the nonce.
