**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Track progress through the resync prefix**

When `resync_chunk` is smaller than the catalog/logging prefix, this explicitly allows the prefix to span chunks, but the only retained cursor described below ranges over subscribed URIs. After emitting a partial prefix, the next iteration therefore cannot determine which catalog or warning event comes next; restarting the prefix can prevent convergence, while advancing directly to URI updates silently skips the remaining hints. Make the per-consumer cursor cover the entire ordered pass, including a prefix phase and index.
