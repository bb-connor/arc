**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bound the closure record before enumerating the ledger**

For a large capability subtree or process tree, these unconstrained vectors grow with every fence, drained operation, retained hold, permit, and allocation, yet the complete record must later be persisted, migrated, and signed as one canonical `Closed` artifact. With no item bound, paging scheme, or committed summary root, a sufficiently large but valid authority space can exceed record or serialization limits and remain stuck in `Draining` after its fences have committed, never producing the promised closure evidence. Store bounded pages with an authenticated aggregate, or enforce a size bound before beginning closure.
