**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Persist restored subscriptions under the v3 envelope**

After A24 has stored a session with subscriptions under the v3 integrity envelope, the next boot runs A17 first and explicitly persists the advanced generation under the v2 HMAC. The v2 envelope's fixed field list does not cover `subscriptions`, so this rewrite must either drop the subscriptions, produce a record that later fails integrity, or leave the subscription list unauthenticated; all three break the promise that subscriptions survive restore. A17 must preserve and authenticate the complete record using v3 whenever the subscription field is present.
