**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make external-source hint posting crash-safe**

When approval or recovery state commits outside the process journal, a host crash after that commit but before this post leaves `hint_revision` unchanged. Because the design adds no durable outbox or per-process source cursor, restart cannot determine that this process missed the change; a subsequent `inspect(after_revision)` can therefore wait or time out with no revision gap and no hint, silently violating H8. Persist a projection cursor or transactional outbox so startup can replay this gap.
