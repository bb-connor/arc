**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Schedule a full follow-up resync after mid-burst lag**

With the new chunking rule, `Lagged` during an active burst proves that this consumer missed notifications from an already emitted chunk, but continuing from `resync_cursor` never sends that chunk's catalog or resource hints again. For example, a consumer that misses the chunk containing resource A and then receives the remaining chunks completes the burst without ever being told to re-read A, and this rule explicitly prevents another resync from being scheduled. Finish the current burst and queue a new full pass, or track the cursor per consumer from the earliest missed position.
