**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Retain expired subscriptions until the end hint is delivered**

On quiet expiry this path posts `SubscriptionEnded` and immediately removes the subscription, but H3/H5 store pending hints in slots owned per live subscription and delivery happens later during `drain`. Removing the entry here either discards the queued end hint or leaves it without the subscription metadata needed to drain it, so the promised quiet-expiry notification, including the restore case below, can silently disappear. Keep a terminalized subscription until its `SubscriptionEnded` hint is delivered, then remove it.
