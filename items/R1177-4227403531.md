**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Route inbox decisions to the cluster leader**

In a clustered trust-control deployment, public submissions are explicitly forwarded to the leader, but approval reads the contacted node's local inbox and executes issuance there without calling `forward_post_to_leader`; denial has the same omission. An operator reaching a follower therefore sees a missing request, or attempts the authority mutation on the wrong node, even though the pending row exists on the leader. Forward approve and deny before accessing the inbox or issuing authority.
