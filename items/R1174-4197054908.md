**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Revalidate standard subscriptions after restore**

When a standard subscription's capability expires or is revoked after this one-time restore check (including while later subscriptions are still being restored), Part A still activates the URI and has no subsequent authority check: the existing `queue_tool_server_event` path gates updates only on URI membership, while H7/H7a revalidation and expiry scheduling belong to deferred Part B. The restored session can therefore continue receiving `resources/updated` notifications after its time-bounded authority has ended. Part A needs its own expiry scheduling and delivery/auth-rotation revalidation, terminalizing the standard session on failure.
