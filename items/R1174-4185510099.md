**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Reject replayed origin-freshness heartbeats**

A serving shard may renew its lease from any fan-out heartbeat that carries an origin head id, but no freshness or anti-replay condition is defined. After the origin commits a stop, a delayed or replayed pre-stop heartbeat can still carry the shard's current replica id and renew the lease rather than exposing that the origin is ahead; repeated replays let the shard continue admitting `Deny` and `Withhold` crossings beyond the claimed lease bound. Renew only from a response bound to the shard's current challenge/poll, or from authenticated monotonically non-replayable heartbeat evidence.
