**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Keep fault-block emission independent of authority availability**

When an otherwise classifiable request is repeated while an active-defense authority is available and then unavailable, R6 changes the response from a deny containing the authority-fault block to a plain deny. That exposes the authority's online status through block presence, directly contradicting A5's configuration-blind emission requirement. Preserve the same externally visible classification while failing closed internally, or explicitly remove and replace A5 with a defined disclosure policy.
