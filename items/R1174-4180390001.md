**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define a cooldown origin for clockless stops**

In the single-operator profile, `SamePrincipalAfter` may be the only resume path, but S4 permits the stop record to commit with `decided_at = Unavailable`. Even after authority time recovers, there is then no durable instant from which to prove that the configured 300-second cooldown elapsed, so fail-closed validation must reject that principal forever and the deployment cannot resume normally. Define a durable cooldown origin that survives restart for clockless stops, or require an explicit alternate threshold/recovery path whenever this policy is configured.
