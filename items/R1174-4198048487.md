<a href="#"><img alt="P1" src="https://greptile-static-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/badges/p1.svg?v=9" align="top"></a> **Resync passes amplify lag**

When two consumers lag on a session with many subscribed URIs, A8 runs a full resync pass for each consumer but broadcasts each pass to both. One consumer’s pass can fill the other’s broadcast window while it is trying to catch up. The per-iteration chunk limit does not bound the combined passes, so recovery traffic can cause further lag and eventually terminate a stream at `resync_max_passes`.
