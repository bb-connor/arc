**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Backfill authority refs before enabling closure fences**

Operations created before the authority-ref index migration have no rows for the dispatch CAS to join against. Scanning them during drain is insufficient when a live coordinator owns the lease: the drain deliberately does not steal it, while that coordinator's `NOT EXISTS` fence check sees no refs and can commit `DispatchCommitted` after the closure fence. Backfill and verify these refs before enabling closure, or keep a legacy predicate in the dispatch CAS until no unindexed operations remain.
