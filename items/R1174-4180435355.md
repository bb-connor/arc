**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Make receipt-log moves idempotent**

A crash after the receipt-log commit but before deletion of the staging row leaves that row eligible for replay. On restart the mover assigns another log sequence and appends the same signed receipt again; the content-table deduplication in section 11.3 covers repeated substructures, not uniqueness of append-only log entries. Use the staging identity as an idempotency key in the receipt log, or atomically record a durable moved marker, so this crash window cannot duplicate audit entries and Merkle leaves.
