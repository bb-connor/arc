**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind quorum resumes to the active roster**

After an operator roster rotates, principals removed from the roster can still sign a new quorum artifact for the current stop epoch while setting `roster_digest` to an older, validly signed roster. Because this rule makes verification signature-only and never requires that digest to equal the roster currently installed for the scope, a quorum of obsolete or compromised keys can authorize `Resume` or `Relax`. Require the artifact's roster generation or digest to match the active scoped deployment roster, or a roster version explicitly pinned by the stopped incident.
