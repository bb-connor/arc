**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Define a lossless parent-state inheritance encoding**

When a parent has multiple origin classes, multiple providers, or accumulated bounded bits, neither inheritance option here preserves its influence exactly: copying the committed observations reuses IDs that I4a binds to the parent's destination (and may be ignored by the global uniqueness key), while the alternative single `InfluenceObservationV1` can carry only one origin and one bit bound. A child can therefore either fail creation or lose part of the parent's taint and incorrectly satisfy `Trusted`, `ProviderOnly`, or a bounded requirement. Re-key every inherited observation for the child, or define an aggregate observation that faithfully carries all parent counters and classes; an unrepresentable state must deny rather than under-taint.
