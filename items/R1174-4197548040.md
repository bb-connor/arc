<a href="#"><img alt="P1" src="https://greptile-static-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/badges/p1.svg?v=9" align="top"></a> **Retirement invalidates reused reservations**

A new resolution attempt in the same scope may reuse an existing `Reserved` ordinal, but the link keeps the original attempt ID and no revision change is specified. The original attempt can therefore retire that link while the newer attempt is minting a capability bound to the ordinal. The newer attempt’s `CreateWorkflow` then conflicts against the retired link, even though it did not abandon the reservation. Give reuse its own ownership or version transition, or prevent distinct attempts from sharing an ordinal.
