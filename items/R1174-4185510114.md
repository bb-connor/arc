**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Flush the terminal hint before cancelling streams**

Writing `Terminal` into the session log and then cancelling every stream does not guarantee that any stream emits the hint: a stream selecting between its event source and the cancellation token may observe cancellation first, or may close before the reserved terminal slot is drained. That contradicts H6's exactly-one terminal-delivery guarantee and leaves clients seeing an unexplained disconnect. Drain or directly flush the terminal hint to each attached stream before signalling cancellation, with an explicit completion ordering.
