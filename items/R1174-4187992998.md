**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Persist evidence needed to verify bypass intent references**

After a readable-journal bypass succeeds, S25 removes the listed entries, while later boots and replicas must still verify every historical chain record. Because `StopEpochV1` retains only each entry's random `StopIntentRef`, not its containment value or authenticated evidence that the entry existed and was read, a verifier can no longer check either requirement in this rule. Treating the missing entry as invalid makes the chain permanently `not_ready`; accepting the reference without checking makes the claimed proof unverifiable. Persist an authenticated entry snapshot or digest in the bypass record so verification remains possible after journal cleanup.
