**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Recompute stranded capacity after the drain**

Computing every `StrandedCapacity` entry at `Fenced` makes the later `Closed` artifact stale. For example, an operation observed as `DispatchCommitted` can complete and settle while its live lease is respected, or can become outcome-unknown and freeze its hold during the drain; similarly, an in-flight knowledge operation can add a pin after the fence. The snapshot therefore cannot truthfully identify `frozen_holds` or final `pinned_artifacts`, yet it is embedded in the signed `Closed` artifact and proposed as a future reclamation precondition. Recompute the final accounting after all ledger entries are terminal or incident-bound, retaining the fenced-time snapshot separately if it is useful.
