**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Bind non-cage confinement records to production receipts**

Section 5 makes `native_launch` the only receipt binding, but Firecracker and process-container records do not produce that field. This verifier rule checks record identity only for receipts that already carry `native_launch`; for every other receipt it merely accepts a permitted generic `tool_origin`. A host can therefore attest an unrelated Firecracker or container launch while the production receipt came from an unconfined execution with the same origin. Require every confinement-covered production receipt to map its operation or admission digest and attempt to exactly one attested `ConfinementRecord`, regardless of backend.
