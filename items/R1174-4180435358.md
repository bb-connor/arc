**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Add events for counterparty-authorized releases**

The design says `MutuallyAgreedUnknown` and `ContractualCaptureWaiver` arrive as events, but neither appears in `AdmissionEvent`, and M6 makes `Terminal(OutcomeUnknownAfterDispatch)` accept only `Tick`. A later counterparty authorization therefore cannot drive the hold release that M7 explicitly permits, leaving unknown-outcome payment holds permanently frozen after migration to this machine. Add typed release-authority events and allow them in the applicable terminal state while keeping the admission terminal itself absorbing.
