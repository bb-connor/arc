**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Block running rollover heads until their artifact is signed**

Fresh evidence after the round-9 signing-obligation fix is the running-head case: an automatic `Rollover` may restate `Running`, commit first, and then fail signing, and these rules leave it committed with only `evidence_pending`. The `Resume`/`Relax` guard never helps because the scope is already running, so calls continue while the current chain contains an unsigned record, contradicting S38's claim that a running head's whole chain is backed by signed artifacts. Sign running rollovers transactionally or force the scope not-ready/stopped until reconciliation signs the pending record.
