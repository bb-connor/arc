**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Do not compensate while a Retry member is requeued**

When a submitted fused intent loses its batch transaction, spec 10 X21 says the writer returns `Retry` and requeues that same member, but this rule treats `Retry` as terminal once an immediate read-back finds no row and releases all in-memory reservations. That read occurs before the queued retry can commit, so the retry may later reach `DispatchCommitted` after invocation, budget, payment, credential, and runtime reservations have been released. The drop reconciler must first cancel or take ownership of the requeued member, or wait for its final non-retry result, before using absence as proof of non-commitment.
