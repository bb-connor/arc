**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Include identifier length in the capacity bound**

An `Identifier` accepts variable-length values up to `max_len`, so its cardinality is the sum of `|charset|^i` over the permitted lengths, not merely `|charset|^max_len`. The stated formula can therefore undercount the external channel: for the documented 65-character set, lengths 1 through 44 require 266 bits, while rounding `44 * log2(65)` gives 265. Because this value becomes the authoritative `ExternalBounded.max_bits`, a context can satisfy a bound despite receiving more attacker-selectable information than the grant permits. Compute the ceiling from the full variable-length cardinality, or require a fixed-length encoding.
