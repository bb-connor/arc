**<sub><sub>![P1 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P1-orange?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Require exact origin equality before shard readiness**

Fresh evidence after the equal-position fork fix is that boot readiness still accepts a replica whose position is greater than the origin's. After an origin whole-volume rollback, a shard retaining a later `Running` resume can therefore satisfy this `at or above` test while the restored origin is at an earlier `Stopped` head; because a shard can never legitimately outrun its origin, treat this as an origin regression and require an exact position-and-digest match before serving.
