**<sub><sub>![P2 Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P2-yellow?style=flat)</sub></sub>  Zeroize the original systemd credential buffer**

When a systemd credential is read successfully, `read_to_string` allocates the seed in an ordinary `String`, and `value.trim().to_string()` makes a second copy before wrapping only that copy in `Zeroizing`. The original `value` is therefore dropped without being wiped, leaving the authority seed in allocator memory; place the initial buffer in a zeroizing wrapper before trimming or read directly into zeroizing bytes.
